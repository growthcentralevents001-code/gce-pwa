# Product

Growth Central Events — GCE

This file is **design-agent context**, not a business-plan duplicate. Canonical business rules remain in Founder Decisions and `docs/core/`. Do not invent commercial, legal, or RBAC rules from this file.

## Product hierarchy

```text
Logixia Solutions Private Limited
└── Growth Central Events — GCE (platform and master brand)
    ├── GCE Connect
    ├── GCE Marketplace
    └── GCE Enterprise
```

GCE is one unified Progressive Web Application, not three disconnected apps. Official domain: growthcentralevents.com.

- **GCE Connect** — structured business networking, membership, Circles, referrals, Lead Assist.
- **GCE Marketplace** — events, offers, Venue Partners, tickets/bookings.
- **GCE Enterprise** — technology-enabled procurement / workflow / financial-control platform with vendor-led fulfilment. GCE is not ordinarily the physical venue/caterer/executor.

## Primary audiences

From canonical role and product docs (`docs/core/35_Role_Taxonomy.md`, `docs/core/01_GCE_Overview.md`, `docs/core/02_Business_Model.md`):

- Event customers discovering and booking Marketplace experiences
- GCE Connect Members and Circle Members
- Connect BDP, Marketplace BDP, and Enterprise BDP partners
- Venue Partners and venue-side representatives
- Enterprise Clients and their authorised representatives
- Operational, Finance, and admin users in role-based workspaces

One GCE account may hold multiple compatible roles. A workspace is not a separate account.

## Product character

GCE is India’s business-growth ecosystem: trusted, structured, and professional. It combines networking, marketplace commerce, and enterprise programmes in one product family.

Positioning that design work must preserve:

- India-first
- Mobile-first / PWA
- Professional and premium, not decorative
- Business/event ecosystem
- Not generic SaaS
- Not a fintech visual identity
- Not a social-media clone

Voice: plain, specific, operationally honest. Do not use filler such as revolutionary, cutting-edge, or game-changing.

## UX expectations

- One visual family across marketing, customer, Connect, partner, Finance, Ops, and Settings shells
- Task completion with minimum friction; productivity over decoration
- Touch-friendly controls; consistent navigation already defined by GCE shells
- Do not invent future features, wallets cash-out, Affiliate products, or inactive commercial layers
