## Connect

```text
Member
  → Logixia/GCE collection (when payments ON)
    → recognised eligible subscription revenue (after exclusions)
      → Connect BDP entitlement = 20% of eligible **attributed** revenue
        → recovery where Commission-Recovery Finance Option
           (max ₹5,000/cycle from earned commission; Recoverable Balance ₹55,000)
          → settlement-eligible → payout (execution OFF)
      → unattributed/organic: no CBDP share; amount remains with GCE (not “pending CBDP”)
```

Pack: Direct ₹50,000 or Finance ₹60,000 (₹5,000 + ₹55,000 recoverable). Target credit at **15** approved+paid members (once).

## Marketplace attributed (valid MBDP)

```text
Customer
  → payment flow (intended Logixia collector — unvalidated)
    → Eligible Marketplace Event Revenue
      → Venue 80
      → MBDP 10 (from GCE’s 20)
      → GCE net 10
```

## Marketplace unattributed

```text
Customer
  → payment flow
    → Venue 80
    → MBDP 0
    → GCE 20
```

Missing 10% is **not** pending MBDP commission (FD-037). Prospective reassignment default.

## Enterprise

```text
Client
  → project/component collection (SOW-specific)
    → GCE eligible platform commission/component
      → EBDP 25% of that eligible **GCE platform commission** (not 25% of project value)
    → vendor/other components (not automatically GCE revenue)
```

Finance co-sign internal if proposed project value **> ₹5,00,000**.
