## Proposed policy points (not final)

- Licence to host, display, promote the listing on GCE.  
- No claim that GCE owns user photos merely by upload — **counsel to draft**.  
- Prohibited: unlawful, infringing, pornographic involving minors, misleading Event capacity/price.  
- Reports via Support; Ops moderation exists (Phase 13); **do not overbuild tooling in Phase 15**.  
- Intermediary safe-harbour **only if** counsel classifies GCE as intermediary for UGC — **not assumed**.
