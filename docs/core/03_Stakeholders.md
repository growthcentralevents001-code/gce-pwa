 Stakeholders

 Overview

The GCE (Growth Central Events) ecosystem consists of multiple stakeholders, each playing a specific role in building, managing, and growing the platform.

Every stakeholder has dedicated responsibilities, permissions, dashboards, workflows, and revenue opportunities. Together, they form a complete Business Growth Ecosystem.

 1\. User

Users are the primary customers of the platform.

They interact with GCE to discover events, connect with businesses, claim offers, purchase memberships, and submit business requirements.

 Explore Events  
 Book Events  
 Purchase Memberships  
 Claim Marketplace Offers  
 Submit Business Requirements  
 Connect with Businesses

 Business Opportunities  
 Marketplace Offers  
 Event Access  
 Business Community

 RoleBased Access

Each stakeholder has a dedicated dashboard with rolebased permissions.

No stakeholder can access another stakeholder's confidential information unless explicitly authorized by the platform.

This ensures:

 Data Privacy  
 Security  
 Accountability  
 Operational Transparency

CBDPs expand networking communities.

Enterprise BDPs acquire corporate business.

Together, these stakeholders create a sustainable, AIpowered Business Growth Ecosystem.
