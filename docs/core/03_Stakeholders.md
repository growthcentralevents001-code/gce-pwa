# Stakeholders

## Authority

**Foundational stakeholders:** FD-001 · **Corporate constitution:** FD-034 · **Roles/RBAC:** FD-023 / `35_Role_Taxonomy.md` · **Circles lifecycle:** FD-024 · **Circles internal governance:** FD-030 / `38_Circle_Architecture.md` · **Dual Circle status mapping:** FD-032 · **Connect BDP commercial:** FD-025 · **Marketplace BDP ops:** FD-033 · **GCE Enterprise:** FD-026 · **AI Lead Assist / Opportunity Desk:** FD-031 / `39_AI_Lead_Assist_Spec.md` · **Commercial numbers:** `36_Commercial_Constants.md`

Prefer approved names: Connect BDP, Marketplace BDP, Enterprise BDP, Enterprise Platform Expert, Circle Member, Enterprise Client, **Governing Body** (legacy: Board of Governance / Circle Board), **Circle Finance Coordinator** (Treasurer is legacy for current governance), **GCE Lead Intelligence and Opportunity Desk**. Legal company: **Logixia Solutions Private Limited**; platform/master brand: **GCE** (FD-034). Circles and platform IP are owned/controlled under Logixia / GCE — stakeholders do not own Circles or verticals. Multi-role via one account is Founder-approved; conflict rules apply (FD-023). Connect BDP does not own territory, Circles, members, or data (FD-025 / FD-030). Marketplace BDP does not permanently own cities or venues and is venue-attribution based (FD-033). Enterprise BDP allocation is client-based; Enterprise BDP and Enterprise Platform Expert do not own clients, projects, or data (FD-026). Governing Body supports Circle operations but does not own Circles, approve final membership/taxonomy, or terminate members independently (FD-030); term six months (FD-032). Opportunity Desk supports lead verification/coordination but does **not** own leads and must not receive hidden personal commission (FD-031). Legacy **PRM** language may map to desk operational support pending role migration. No BDP/member/Venue Partner may bind Logixia by default (FD-034).

Overview

The GCE (Growth Central Events) ecosystem consists of multiple stakeholders, each playing a specific role in building, managing, and growing the platform.

Every stakeholder has dedicated responsibilities, permissions, dashboards, workflows, and revenue opportunities. Together, they form a complete Business Growth Ecosystem.

 Stakeholder Ecosystem

Every stakeholder contributes to the overall growth of GCE.

Users create demand.

Circle Members build business relationships.

Venue Partners generate marketplace activity.

Connect BDPs expand networking communities.

Marketplace BDPs grow the marketplace.

Enterprise BDPs acquire corporate business.

PRMs / Opportunity Desk operators validate and coordinate business opportunities (do not own leads — FD-031).

RMs support the ecosystem.

Board of Governance / Governing Body maintains circle quality (supports ops; platform retains final membership and taxonomy authority — FD-030).

Platform Administrators manage the complete platform.

Together, these stakeholders create a sustainable, AIpowered Business Growth Ecosystem.
