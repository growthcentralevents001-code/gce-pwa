 Connect Business Development Partner (CBDP)

 Overview

The Connect Business Development Partner (CBDP) is the primary business development role responsible for expanding the GCE Connect ecosystem within assigned cities and business circles.

A CBDP focuses on building strong networking communities by onboarding members, creating new circles, managing memberships, and ensuring consistent business growth.

CBDPs play a key role in increasing GCE's recurring membership revenue and strengthening local business communities.

 Primary Objectives

The main objectives of a CBDP are:

 Build Business Circles  
 Increase Membership Sales  
 Expand GCE Connect  
 Develop Local Business Communities  
 Improve Member Retention  
 Support Business Networking  
 Generate Recurring Revenue

 Core Responsibilities

A CBDP is responsible for:

 Recruiting New Members  
 Creating New Business Circles  
 Conducting Member Meetings  
 Promoting GCE Memberships  
 Monitoring Circle Performance  
 Supporting Members  
 Coordinating with Relationship Managers  
 Maintaining Business Standards  
 Growing the Local GCE Community

 Business Circle Allocation

Each CBDP can manage:

 Maximum 5 Business Circles

Each circle operates independently but follows the same platform business rules.

 Commission Model

CBDPs earn incentives based on membership sales and business growth.

Current Commission

 20%

Commission calculations are managed automatically through the platform dashboard.

 Franchise Model

Every approved CBDP receives one franchise allocation.

If a CBDP wishes to expand beyond the assigned franchise, an additional franchise reservation can be purchased.

Additional franchise allocation follows the platform's franchise policy.

 Finance Option

If an eligible applicant cannot pay the complete franchise fee, GCE may provide a finance option through an approved finance partner.

Key points:

 Initial Training Fee is mandatory.  
 Finance approval depends on eligibility.  
 Repayment starts after the initial business period.  
 Financing terms are managed according to the official franchise policy.
