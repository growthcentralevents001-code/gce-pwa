## GCE Enterprise positioning

GCE Enterprise is a technology-enabled, stakeholder-fulfilled corporate event procurement, matching, coordination, workflow, and financial-control platform.

GCE is an IT platform. GCE does **not** directly execute physical events and does not itself become the venue operator, caterer, production vendor, decorator, AV provider, transport provider, security provider, hospitality provider, manpower provider, or physical event-management contractor.

Physical fulfilment is performed by approved stakeholders and vendors.

GCE’s platform role includes client onboarding, requirement capture, project creation, Enterprise BDP attribution, Enterprise Platform Expert assignment, service-requirement breakdown, vendor discovery/verification/shortlisting, quotation collection and comparison, client approvals, work-order workflows, payment tracking, settlement controls, commission calculation, Vendor Opportunity Fee tracking, readiness tracking, completion evidence, ratings, dispute escalation, audit trails, notifications, dashboards, and platform records (FD-026).

## Fulfilment providers

Enterprise requirements may be fulfilled by:

- Approved GCE Connect Circle members
- Approved Venue Partners
- Approved Marketplace participants
- Existing verified GCE vendors
- Verified external vendors when suitable GCE stakeholders are unavailable
- Other approved GCE ecosystem participants

The selected venue or vendor remains responsible for physical delivery. External vendors must not bypass verification.

## Minimum project value

GCE Enterprise will not accept an event order below **₹1,00,000** eligible project value (excludes GST and other statutory taxes).

Projects below ₹1,00,000 may be rejected, referred to another GCE vertical, combined into a larger requirement, or accepted only through special authorised approval (FD-026).

---

## Enterprise Client allocation

Enterprise BDP allocation is **client-based**, not territory-based. Enterprise BDPs do **not** receive permanent geographic territory ownership.

An Enterprise BDP may source clients across cities, subject to valid attribution, active-client capacity, platform approval, client consent, and duplicate-client controls.

An active client should generally require verified organisation, authorised representative, formal Enterprise BDP attribution, qualified commercial relationship, and an open opportunity, active proposal, live project, recently completed protected project, or another approved active state.

Raw leads, unverified contacts, or business cards do **not** automatically consume active-client capacity.

Enterprise BDP does **not** own Enterprise Clients, projects, vendor data, client data, leads, proposals, financial records, commission records, project history, or platform IP (FD-026).

## Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack

The commercial unit is the **Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack**.

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Active-client capacity | Up to **30** per pack |
| Additional capacity | Requires a **separate** Franchise Pack |
| Standard person / entity limit | Maximum **two** active packs (**60** clients); more requires special platform approval |
| Direct-payment fee | **₹30,000** upfront per pack (one-time; non-refundable after training or activation; not a security deposit; no launch discount) |
| Financed package | Total **₹36,000** · Initial **₹5,000** · Recoverable **₹31,000** · Max monthly recovery **₹5,000** from earned and approved Enterprise BDP commission only |

Each Franchise Pack is a separate commercial and operating unit (Pack ID, assigned Enterprise BDP, activation/payment status, client-capacity tracking, performance target, commission records, finance-recovery records where applicable, audit history).

Finance recovery: recover the lower of ₹5,000 or available approved commission; no automatic monthly cash-shortfall demand; unrecovered balance carries forward; no interest beyond the fixed ₹36,000 package value; deductions must be visible in dashboard and ledger; exit/suspension does not automatically erase the balance; post-exit recovery follows the signed agreement (FD-026).

Numeric summary: `36_Commercial_Constants.md`.

## Client capacity and targets

Per Franchise Pack:

- Maximum active-client capacity: **30**
- Monthly target: **₹3,00,000** eligible Enterprise event revenue
- Rolling three-month target: **₹9,00,000** eligible Enterprise event revenue

Targets apply separately to every active Franchise Pack.

Target revenue must be successfully collected, linked to valid Enterprise projects, correctly attributed, and excluding GST/statutory taxes, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, cancelled projects, and uncollected invoices.

Do **not** count proposal value, verbal commitments, unapproved quotations, purchase orders without eligible collection, tax amounts, cancelled projects, or uncollected invoices.

## Enterprise BDP responsibilities and authority limits

Responsibilities may include lead generation, client qualification, onboarding support, initial requirement capture, requirement-brief coordination, client communication, proposal presentation, commercial coordination, contract / purchase-order / payment follow-up, relationship management, repeat-business development, post-project feedback, accurate platform records, and coordination with the Enterprise Platform Expert.

Enterprise BDP may **not** independently:

- Approve quotations
- Change the platform commission
- Offer unauthorised discounts
- Sign contracts for GCE
- Finally select vendors
- Approve vendor payments, refunds, or settlements
- Approve personal commission
- Move platform funds or alter financial records
- Promise guaranteed income or project acceptance
- Execute physical event services
- Access unrelated client or vendor data

## Enterprise Platform Expert

Official role: **GCE Enterprise Platform Expert**. Short name: **Enterprise Platform Expert**.

The Enterprise Platform Expert is an internal GCE employee or a directly controlled specialist under controls equivalent to an authorised internal user.

The role is **not** an independent franchise role, external unrestricted vendor, territory owner, client owner, or project owner.

Required controls include confidentiality, data protection, RBAC, project-scoped access, audit monitoring, conflict-of-interest disclosure, immediate access revocation, and GCE supervision (FD-023 / FD-026).

Responsibilities include reviewing the Event Requirement Brief, breaking the project into service requirements, searching approved GCE platform data, identifying Connect Circle members / Venue Partners / Marketplace participants / verified vendors, preparing shortlists, inviting and comparing quotations, preparing consolidated proposal inputs, coordinating approval workflows, tracking readiness and completion evidence, escalating service issues, coordinating digital project closure, and maintaining project records.

The Expert must **not** physically execute events, collect money personally, approve personal compensation, manipulate quotations, hide competing vendors, add unverified vendors without approval, change pricing without authority, sign contracts for GCE, approve refunds or settlements independently, accept vendor kickbacks, access unrelated project data, or guarantee vendor performance.

Compensation may use fixed salary, fixed retainer, project-based incentive, or approved performance bonus. Exact amounts and any Vendor Opportunity Fee share for the Expert are **unresolved** — do not invent. No fixed percentage of Vendor Opportunity Fees is approved for the Expert.

Recommended launch capacity: maximum **10** active standard Enterprise projects per Expert. Weighted capacity for major or multi-city projects remains unresolved.

## Requirement and vendor matching

Every Enterprise project should use an **Event Requirement Brief** (client objective, event type, audience, attendance, city/cities, dates, budget, required services, branding, technical, food/hospitality, security, accessibility, reporting, approval deadlines, and related items).

The project should be broken into **Service Requirements** (venue, catering, AV/production, stage/branding, registration, photography, transport, security, entertainment, accommodation, speakers, trainers, other approved services).

**Vendor priority — first:** qualified Connect Circle members, approved Venue Partners, approved Marketplace participants, existing verified GCE vendors.
**Second:** verified external vendors only when no suitable GCE stakeholder is available, stakeholders decline, capacity is insufficient, the requirement is specialised, or pricing/compliance is unsuitable.

Approved vendor-selection workflow:

```text
Requirement
  → Vendor Search
  → Vendor Verification
  → Vendor Shortlist
  → Quotation Invitation
  → Comparative Commercial Review
  → Platform Expert Recommendation
  → Client Approval
  → Work Order
```

The Enterprise Platform Expert recommends; the Expert does not have unrestricted unilateral approval authority.

## Project lifecycle

```text
Lead → Qualified → Client Verification → Requirement Discovery
  → Event Requirement Brief → Enterprise Platform Expert Assignment
  → Service Breakdown → Vendor Search → Vendor Shortlist
  → Quotation Collection → Commercial Comparison → Proposal Draft
  → Internal Review → Client Review → Negotiation
  → Contract / Purchase Order → Initial Payment → Work Orders
  → Readiness Tracking → Vendor-Led Physical Execution
  → Completion Evidence → Client Confirmation
  → Vendor Settlement → Commission Distribution → Project Closure
```

Additional states may include On Hold, Change Requested, Cancelled, Disputed, Suspended, Reopened. Exact technical enum names remain Pending Technical Design.

## Multi-city projects

A centrally contracted multi-city client normally counts as **one** active Enterprise Client (not one slot per city) when the contracting organisation and procurement authority are the same. Separate legal entities may count separately if they have separate contracting, billing, procurement, or approval structures.

Architecture: one Enterprise Client · one attributed Enterprise BDP · one **Master Project** · one Lead Enterprise Platform Expert · multiple **City Units** · multiple Service Requirements · separate vendors per city where required · one consolidated client view.

GCE controls the digital workflow, not physical event execution.

## GCE platform commission

Standard: **20%** of eligible Enterprise event revenue.

Reduced approved range: **15%–19%** for qualifying strategic projects (large projects, repeat clients, annual contracts, multi-city engagements, competitive tenders, strategic institutional clients). Reduced commission is **not** automatic. Below **15%** requires special Founder or senior-authority approval.

Eligible revenue excludes GST, statutory taxes, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, failed payments, complimentary services, unapproved pass-through amounts, fraudulent transactions, and amounts not actually received.

---

## Enterprise BDP commission

Approved commission: flat **25%** of the eligible platform commission actually earned by GCE from the attributed Enterprise project. There is **no** tiered Enterprise BDP commission at launch.

```text
Eligible Enterprise Event Revenue × Approved GCE Platform Commission Rate
  = GCE Platform Commission

GCE Platform Commission × 25%
  = Enterprise BDP Commission
```

Illustrative examples at ₹10,00,000 eligible event revenue (not guaranteed income):

| Platform rate | Platform commission | Enterprise BDP (25%) | GCE retained |
|---------------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| 20% | ₹2,00,000 | ₹50,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| 15% | ₹1,50,000 | ₹37,500 | ₹1,12,500 |

Commission becomes earned proportionately only after client payment is collected, related milestone is approved, platform commission becomes settlement-eligible, attribution is valid, and no material refund, dispute, or chargeback exists.

Numeric summary: `36_Commercial_Constants.md`. Settlement principles: FD-020 / FD-021.

## Repeat clients, dormancy, and reassignment

- Repeat-project commission: Enterprise BDP continues earning **25%** of eligible platform commission while active, materially involved, and validly attributed.
- Client protection: recommended **12 months** from the most recent successfully completed attributed project.
- Dormant review: **90** consecutive days without meaningful client activity may trigger review.
- Before release: notify Enterprise BDP, allow evidence of engagement, conduct platform review, preserve historical attribution.
- Duplicate-client disputes consider existing verified attribution, documented first meaningful engagement, approved client introduction, client confirmation, and platform decision. An email address alone does not create ownership.
- No Enterprise BDP may privately sell, trade, or transfer client attribution.
- After reassignment: earned/approved commission remains payable subject to valid deductions; pending commission remains under review; future commission follows the approved reassignment date; historical earned commission must not be silently transferred. Treatment of projects spanning the reassignment date remains unresolved.

## Payment and settlement

Standard Enterprise payment structure: **30%** at project confirmation · **40%** at approved readiness or execution milestone · **30%** after completion. This is a standard model, not an inflexible rule. Alternative schedules may be approved based on project size, vendor commitments, duration, client credit profile, procurement rules, and risk (FD-021 / FD-026).

Client payment does **not** automatically mean vendor settlement is payable, Enterprise BDP commission is payable, Vendor Opportunity Fee is earned, refund risk has ended, or the milestone is approved.

Vendor settlement requires valid work order, deliverable verification, completion evidence, applicable approval, invoice and compliance verification, dispute/deduction review, and settlement eligibility.

## Performance management

Formal performance review may be triggered when:

- Monthly target is missed for two consecutive months
- Rolling three-month target is missed
- Client servicing materially falls below policy

Approved progressive process:

1. Formal performance review
2. Written corrective plan
3. Sixty-day improvement period
4. Additional training or supervision
5. Restriction on onboarding new clients
6. Reduction or reassignment of active-client capacity
7. Suspension or cancellation after persistent failure

Serious misconduct may bypass this sequence. Do **not** create automatic cancellation after one or two weak months.

Exact performance-score formula remains unresolved.

---

## Serious misconduct

Immediate suspension or termination may apply for Enterprise BDP misconduct including unauthorised collection, false quotation, unauthorised discount, contract misrepresentation, client-data misuse, commission manipulation, fraud, fake projects, conflict concealment, brand misuse, platform-security abuse, and deliberate false reporting.

For Enterprise Platform Expert: data misuse, quotation manipulation, vendor kickbacks, hidden conflicts, unauthorised access, settlement manipulation, fraud, platform-security abuse, deliberate false reporting, and undisclosed related-vendor favouritism (FD-026).

## Long-term vision

The Enterprise BDP program builds a nationwide corporate acquisition network that attributes Enterprise Clients to Franchise Packs, coordinates digital procurement and fulfilment workflows through Enterprise Platform Experts and verified stakeholders, and earns eligible platform-commission share — without ownership of clients, projects, territory, or platform assets, and without GCE directly executing physical events.
