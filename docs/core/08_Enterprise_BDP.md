# Enterprise Business Development Partner (Enterprise BDP)

## Authority

**Platform model:** FD-001 · **Settlement milestones:** FD-021 · **RBAC:** FD-023 · **Commercial numbers:** `36_Commercial_Constants.md`

Approved role name: **GCE Enterprise Business Development Partner (Enterprise BDP)**.

Enterprise is **project-driven**, not a Circle-membership product (FD-001). Standard Founder-approved milestone model: **30% / 40% / 30%** unless a separately approved contract specifies otherwise (FD-021). Enterprise BDP commission % remains undocumented pending commercial Founder Decision. Enterprise BDP must not automatically release payments or settlements (FD-023). **Enterprise Client** is a distinct role from Enterprise BDP.

Overview

The Enterprise Business Development Partner (Enterprise BDP) is responsible for expanding the **GCE Enterprise** division by acquiring corporate clients, institutions, government organizations, SMEs, and large businesses requiring professional event management and enterprise business solutions.

Unlike Connect BDPs and Marketplace BDPs, Enterprise BDPs primarily focus on highvalue business projects rather than memberships or marketplace listings.

The **GCE Enterprise** division generates revenue through corporate events, conferences, exhibitions, annual functions, product launches, employee engagement programs, and complete event execution services.

 Franchise Model

Anyone interested in building an Enterprise business can apply for an Enterprise BDP Franchise.

 Franchise Fee

 See **`36_Commercial_Constants.md`** (Enterprise BDP → Enterprise franchise fee).

 Finance Option

If an applicant cannot pay the complete franchise fee, GCE may provide financing through an approved finance partner.

 Finance Rules

 Initial Training Fee is mandatory.
 Remaining amount can be financed.
 EMI deductions begin after the initial business period.
 Finance approval depends on company policy and applicant eligibility.

 Eligibility

Any individual may become an Enterprise BDP if they:

 Complete the required training.
 Meet GCE's eligibility criteria.
 Pay the required franchise fee (or qualify for finance).
 Demonstrate the ability to acquire enterprise clients.

 Client Acquisition Requirement

Enterprise BDPs should be capable of acquiring business opportunities from corporate organizations.

The expected client acquisition capacity is monitored through dashboard analytics and business performance rather than fixed sales volume alone.

 LongTerm Vision

The Enterprise Business Development Partner program is designed to build a nationwide corporate sales network capable of acquiring and managing enterprise business opportunities.

Enterprise BDPs will act as GCE's official corporate representatives, helping organizations plan, manage, and execute professional events while expanding the GCE Enterprise ecosystem across India.
