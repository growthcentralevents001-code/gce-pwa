# FD-001 — GCE Business Model

**Decision ID:** FD-001
**Title:** GCE Business Model
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** Foundational Business Constitution
**Authority Level:** Highest business authority, subject only to later Founder Decisions that explicitly amend or supersede this file
**Platform:** Growth Central Events (GCE)
**Official Domain:** `growthcentralevents.com`

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision establishes the foundational business model, platform structure, stakeholder model, commercial direction, and operating principles of Growth Central Events (GCE).

This file defines the approved high-level business architecture of GCE. Detailed rules for finance, settlement, membership, permissions, Circle lifecycle, commissions, territory, AI, workflows, security, and engineering implementation must be documented in separate Founder Decisions and canonical documents.

Cursor, developers, architects, and documentation contributors must treat this file as the primary source of truth for the GCE business model.

---

## 2. Platform Identity

Growth Central Events (GCE) is a unified hybrid event, business networking, marketplace, and enterprise-solutions platform.

GCE must be designed and operated as one platform, not as separate disconnected applications.

The platform combines:

- Business networking
- Membership-based business communities
- Events
- Referrals
- Marketplace listings
- Marketplace offers and campaigns
- Venue and business participation
- Corporate and enterprise events
- Business-development partnerships
- Payments, commissions, settlements, and internal ledgers
- Future AI-assisted business matching and validation

The official platform name is:

> **Growth Central Events (GCE)**

The official domain is:

> **growthcentralevents.com**

---

## 3. Approved Platform Verticals

GCE consists of three primary verticals.

### 3.1 GCE Connect

GCE Connect is the membership-driven business networking and professional community vertical.

Its approved purpose includes:

- GCE Connect Circles
- Structured business networking
- Business referrals
- Protected business specializations
- Circle meetings
- Masterclasses
- Member development
- Circle governance
- Business collaboration
- Cross-city and cross-district opportunities, subject to approved access rules
- Future AI-assisted business lead matching

GCE Connect is the flagship community and relationship-building vertical of the platform.

### 3.2 GCE Marketplace

GCE Marketplace is the business, venue, event, offer, booking, shopping, and discovery vertical.

Its approved purpose includes:

- Venue and business onboarding
- Marketplace event listings
- Marketplace offer campaigns
- Customer discovery
- Event booking
- Offer redemption
- Payment-enabled transactions
- Venue dashboards
- Campaign management
- Customer trust and business performance systems
- Marketplace commissions and settlements

Standard Marketplace listings and paid Marketplace commercial products must remain clearly distinguished.

### 3.3 GCE Enterprise

GCE Enterprise is the corporate and B2B project-driven vertical.

Its approved purpose includes:

- Corporate events
- Business conferences
- Training programmes
- Employee engagement
- Vendor-supported projects
- Corporate solutions
- Enterprise quotations
- Milestone-based execution
- Enterprise client management
- Enterprise business-development opportunities

GCE Enterprise is project-driven and must not be treated as a Circle-membership product.

---

## 4. Unified Platform Principle

The three GCE verticals must operate under one unified platform architecture.

The approved principle is:

> **One Platform. One GCE Account. Multiple Approved Roles. Multiple Role-Based Workspaces.**

The platform must not require separate user accounts for GCE Connect, GCE Marketplace, and GCE Enterprise.

A user may participate in one or more verticals through the same GCE account, subject to:

- Eligibility
- Verification
- Role approval
- Conflict-of-interest rules
- Permissions
- Commercial requirements
- Applicable Founder Decisions

---

## 5. Identity and Workspace Model

Every person or business representative should have one primary GCE account.

The platform may assign one or more approved roles to the same account.

Each approved role may provide access to a separate role-based workspace.

Examples may include:

- GCE Connect Member Workspace
- Connect BDP Workspace
- Marketplace BDP Workspace
- Enterprise BDP Workspace
- Venue Partner Workspace
- Circle Governance Workspace
- Platform Operations Workspace
- Platform Administration Workspace

A role-based workspace is not a separate account.

Role assignment, compatibility, permissions, and restrictions must be governed by the approved RBAC Founder Decision.

---

## 6. Core Stakeholder Categories

The GCE ecosystem may include the following stakeholder categories.

### 6.1 Platform Users

Users who register on GCE to discover, book, attend, purchase, participate, or interact with platform services.

### 6.2 GCE Connect Members

Verified business or professional members who participate in GCE Connect and may be allocated to a GCE Connect Circle.

A general GCE membership and a GCE Connect Circle seat are separate business concepts.

### 6.3 Connect Business Development Partners

Independent GCE business partners responsible for developing, managing, and growing approved GCE Connect operations and Circles within their assigned scope.

The approved current role name is:

> **GCE Connect Business Development Partner**
> **Short name: Connect BDP**

### 6.4 Marketplace Business Development Partners

Independent GCE business partners responsible for onboarding, supporting, and managing approved venue and business relationships in GCE Marketplace.

The approved current role name is:

> **GCE Marketplace Business Development Partner**
> **Short name: Marketplace BDP**

### 6.5 Enterprise Business Development Partners

Independent GCE business partners responsible for enterprise client acquisition, enterprise opportunities, and approved enterprise business-development activities.

The approved current role name is:

> **GCE Enterprise Business Development Partner**
> **Short name: Enterprise BDP**

### 6.6 Venue Partners and Marketplace Businesses

Independent merchants, venue owners, service providers, event organisers, and businesses participating in GCE Marketplace.

They are not employees of GCE merely because they use the platform or are onboarded by a Marketplace BDP.

### 6.7 Enterprise Clients

Corporate, institutional, organisational, or business clients purchasing or requesting enterprise services through GCE Enterprise.

### 6.8 Circle Governance Roles

Approved governance roles operating inside a GCE Connect Circle, including the Board of Governance and other approved Circle leadership roles.

Circle governance roles do not own the Circle or the GCE platform.

### 6.9 Platform Operations and Administration

Authorised GCE personnel or approved operational roles responsible for platform governance, review, verification, support, compliance, finance, taxonomy, and administration.

---

## 7. Business Partner Model

Connect BDPs, Marketplace BDPs, and Enterprise BDPs are independent business-development partners operating under separate vertical-specific business models.

They must not automatically receive authority over another GCE vertical.

Each BDP role must have its own:

- Eligibility
- Appointment process
- Training
- Commercial terms
- Targets
- Commission rules
- Workspace
- Permissions
- Territory or capacity rules
- Performance review
- Suspension and termination rules

A BDP relationship with GCE does not create ownership of GCE, a Circle, a venue, a client, platform data, or platform intellectual property.

---

## 8. Multi-Role Principle

A single GCE account may hold more than one compatible role.

However:

- Roles must be separately approved.
- Permissions must be separately granted.
- Commercial ledgers must remain separately identifiable.
- Conflicts of interest must be prevented.
- A role in one vertical must not automatically grant authority in another vertical.

The approved conflict rule includes:

> A user must not simultaneously act as a GCE Connect Circle Member and as the Connect BDP responsible for the same Circle or directly conflicting Connect structure.

Detailed role compatibility must be governed by the RBAC and role-taxonomy documentation.

## 9. Revenue Architecture

GCE may earn revenue through multiple approved categories.

### 9.1 Current or Launch-Phase Revenue Categories

- Membership subscriptions
- Marketplace transaction commissions
- Marketplace offer or campaign charges
- Event revenue and commissions
- Enterprise project and service revenue
- Business-partner franchise, certification, training, licence, or access fees where approved
- AI lead validation or related approved platform services
- Other approved transaction or service charges

### 9.2 Future Revenue Categories

Future revenue may include:

- Advertising
- Sponsored visibility
- Premium listings
- Premium business subscriptions
- Analytics and business insights
- Additional business services
- Financial or loyalty services
- Other Founder-approved monetisation products

Future revenue categories must not be treated as active until separately approved.

---

## 10. Commercial Separation Principle

Commercial rules must remain separated by vertical and transaction type.

The platform must not assume that one commission percentage, settlement rule, or pricing structure applies universally to all GCE products.

Examples requiring separate approved rules include:

- GCE Connect membership revenue
- GCE Connect event revenue
- GCE Marketplace standard listings
- Marketplace event bookings
- Marketplace offer campaigns
- Enterprise projects
- BDP commissions
- Franchise or training recovery
- Refunds
- Chargebacks
- Taxes
- Rewards
- Promotional credits

Detailed commercial rules must be maintained in the appropriate Founder Decisions and canonical commercial documents.

---

## 11. Wallet and Ledger Principle

The GCE platform should present a simple, unified user-facing financial experience where appropriate.

Internally, the platform must maintain separate auditable ledgers for different financial purposes.

A visible GCE Wallet must not be treated as a single undifferentiated accounting balance.

Detailed ledger, escrow, settlement, refund, commission, reward, recovery, and tax rules must be governed by the approved Financial and Wallet Architecture Founder Decision.

---

## 12. Ownership and Control Principles

The following are approved foundational ownership principles:

- The GCE platform belongs to GCE.
- GCE intellectual property belongs to GCE or its authorised legal owner.
- GCE Connect Circles are GCE platform assets.
- A Connect BDP manages and grows a Circle but does not own it.
- Circle members participate in a Circle but do not own it.
- A Circle Board governs approved Circle matters but does not own the Circle.
- Venue Partners remain independent businesses.
- Enterprise clients remain independent clients.
- BDPs do not own platform users, platform data, venue relationships, Circle records, or enterprise records.
- GCE retains final platform authority, subject to applicable law and approved contracts.

---

## 13. Data and Audit Principle

GCE must maintain auditable records for important business actions.

Important records must not be deleted merely because:

- A membership expires
- A role is removed
- A BDP relationship ends
- A Circle is merged or archived
- A venue relationship is reassigned
- A project is completed
- A dispute is closed

Retention, access, privacy, and deletion must follow approved legal, security, and data-governance policies.

GCE must be developed as a scalable platform capable of supporting:

- Multiple cities
- Multiple Circles
- Multiple BDPs
- Multiple venues
- Multiple enterprise clients
- Multiple user roles
- Multi-vertical participation

## 19. Prohibited Interpretations

This Founder Decision must not be interpreted to mean:

- Each GCE vertical requires a separate account.
- Every user automatically receives access to all three verticals.
- Every BDP may operate in all verticals.
- One commercial percentage applies to every transaction.
- A BDP owns a Circle, venue, member, or enterprise client.
- A platform membership automatically guarantees a Circle seat.
- A Circle Board has unrestricted platform authority.
- Cursor may fill business-rule gaps by assumption.
- Future revenue ideas are already approved for launch.
- Engineering implementation may override Founder Decisions.
