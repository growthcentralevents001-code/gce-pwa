# FD-024 — GCE Connect Circle Lifecycle

**Decision ID:** FD-024
**Title:** GCE Connect Circle Lifecycle
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** GCE Connect Operating Constitution
**Authority Level:** Founder Decision
**Platform:** Growth Central Events (GCE)
**Applies To:** GCE Connect Circles, Connect BDPs, Circle Members, Circle Governance, RM, PRM, and Platform Administration

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision defines the complete approved lifecycle of a GCE Connect Circle.

It establishes:

- Circle ownership
- Circle creation authority
- Platform validation
- Founding-member requirements
- Formation and activation
- Maximum Circle capacity
- Circle expansion
- Lifecycle statuses
- Circle naming
- Circle merger
- Circle split
- Circle suspension
- Circle archival
- Historical preservation
- Circle Health Score
- Role boundaries
- Unresolved matters that must not be invented

This Founder Decision is the highest authority for the GCE Connect Circle lifecycle.

---

## 2. Definition of a GCE Connect Circle

A **GCE Connect Circle** is a structured, verified, platform-governed business networking unit operating inside GCE Connect.

Its purpose is to help approved business owners and professionals:

- Build trusted business relationships
- Exchange business referrals
- Participate in structured meetings
- Develop practical business skills
- Collaborate with complementary businesses
- Occupy protected business-specialization seats
- Grow their businesses through a governed ecosystem
- Build cross-city and cross-district opportunities where approved

A Circle is not an informal social or networking group.

It is a GCE platform entity with:

- A unique Circle identity
- A defined geographic association
- A defined lifecycle status
- A maximum membership capacity
- Protected business-specialization seats
- Governance roles
- Meeting records
- Referral records
- Performance records
- Compliance records
- Audit history

---

## 3. Circle Ownership

Every GCE Connect Circle belongs to GCE.

A Circle is not owned by:

- The Connect BDP
- Circle Members
- The Board of Governance
- The Chairperson
- RM
- PRM
- Any Circle officer
- Any local organisation or association

The approved ownership model is:

```text
GCE
  ↓
Owns and controls the Circle as a platform asset

Connect BDP
  ↓
Initiates, develops, supports, and grows the Circle

Board of Governance
  ↓
Governs approved internal Circle activities

Circle Members
  ↓
Participate and occupy approved business-specialization seats
```

Circle ownership remains with GCE even when:

- The Connect BDP resigns
- The Connect BDP is suspended or terminated
- Board members change
- Members leave
- Territory structures change
- The Circle is merged
- The Circle is archived

---

## 4. Circle Creation Authority

An approved **GCE Connect Business Development Partner** may initiate a new GCE Connect Circle within the assigned territory or approved operating scope.

The current approved short role name is:

> **Connect BDP**

The Connect BDP may initiate the Circle creation process but cannot independently activate the Circle.

The approved creation flow is:

```text
Connect BDP identifies a viable Circle opportunity
        ↓
Connect BDP submits a Circle creation request
        ↓
GCE Platform validates the request
        ↓
Request is approved, rejected, or returned for clarification
        ↓
Approved Circle enters Formation status
```

The Circle Board does not create a Circle.

The Board governs a Circle only after the required governance structure is established.

---

## 5. Circle Creation Request

A Circle creation request should provide enough information for platform evaluation.

The request should be capable of recording:

- Proposed geography
- Proposed Circle naming inputs
- Responsible Connect BDP
- Expected founding-member pipeline
- Initial specialization demand
- Local market viability
- Expected formation timeline
- Possible conflict with existing Circles
- Relevant compliance information
- Platform review notes

The exact mandatory form fields are not finalised in this Founder Decision.

Cursor and developers must not invent additional mandatory requirements.

---

## 6. Platform Validation

Before a Circle enters Formation status, the GCE Platform should validate:

- Connect BDP appointment and authority
- Assigned territory or operating scope
- Need for a new Circle
- Existing Circle occupancy
- Local business demand
- Operational viability
- Potential category or specialization conflicts
- Compliance status
- Platform capacity and restrictions

Platform validation may result in:

- Approved
- Rejected
- Returned for clarification
- Held for further review

A Connect BDP cannot bypass platform validation.

---

## 7. Circle Lifecycle Statuses

Every Circle must always have one defined lifecycle status.

The approved statuses are:

1. Draft
2. Formation
3. Pending Activation
4. Active
5. Growth
6. Full Capacity
7. Mature
8. Under Review
9. Suspended
10. Merged
11. Archived

These statuses support:

- Dashboards
- Permissions
- Workflow automation
- Notifications
- Reporting
- Compliance
- Expansion decisions
- Performance monitoring
- Historical tracking

---

## 8. Draft Status

A Circle is in Draft status when:

- The Circle request is being prepared
- Required information is incomplete
- Formal platform approval has not been received
- Founding-member onboarding has not officially begun under that Circle

A Draft Circle is not an operational Circle.

---

## 9. Formation Status

A Circle enters Formation status after the platform approves the creation request.

During Formation:

- Founding members may be recruited
- Businesses may be verified
- Membership applications may be processed
- Specializations may be reviewed
- Seats may be checked or reserved
- Governance preparation may begin
- An initial meeting may be planned

A Circle in Formation is not yet officially operational.

---

## 10. Pending Activation Status

A Circle enters Pending Activation when the formation requirements are substantially complete and final platform approval is pending.

This stage may include:

- Minimum founding-member threshold achieved
- Founding-member verification substantially completed
- Seat validation completed
- Governance preparation completed or substantially completed
- Initial meeting planned
- Final platform review pending

Pending Activation does not equal Active status.

---

## 11. Active Status

A Circle becomes Active only after the GCE Platform grants final activation approval.

An Active Circle may:

- Conduct official GCE Connect meetings
- Maintain active Circle Members
- Use approved referral systems
- Operate governance functions
- Maintain attendance and performance records
- Use approved Circle dashboards and workflows

The Connect BDP cannot independently change a Circle to Active status.

---

## 12. Growth Status

An activated Circle remains in Growth status while it is building toward full capacity.

The approved Growth range is:

> **15 to 39 active members**

Growth activities may include:

- Member recruitment
- Seat filling
- Waitlist management
- Member retention
- Referral engagement
- Meeting-quality improvement
- Governance strengthening
- Circle performance monitoring

Growth must not compromise verification, specialization exclusivity, governance, or compliance.

---

## 13. Full Capacity Status

A Circle reaches Full Capacity when it has:

> **40 active members**

Forty active members is the maximum physical membership capacity of one GCE Connect Circle.

A Circle must not exceed 40 active physical members.

When a Circle reaches Full Capacity:

- New members may be waitlisted
- Another suitable Circle may be offered
- A new Circle may be formed
- A seat may be offered only after an existing seat becomes available

Any change to the 40-member limit requires another Founder Decision.

---

## 14. Mature Status

Mature status represents a stable, established Circle with consistent operations and governance.

The exact measurable criteria for Mature status have not yet been Founder Approved.

Cursor and developers must not invent:

- Required operating period
- Required Health Score
- Attendance threshold
- Referral threshold
- Renewal threshold
- Automatic transition rules

---

## 15. Under Review Status

A Circle may enter Under Review status because of:

- Declining performance
- Governance concerns
- Compliance concerns
- Membership decline
- Complaint patterns
- Operational instability
- Financial concerns
- Audit concerns
- Repeated policy violations

Under Review does not automatically mean Suspended.

The review should determine whether the Circle:

- Returns to normal operation
- Receives corrective instructions
- Remains under monitoring
- Is suspended
- Is merged
- Is archived

The exact review workflow and SLA are not finalised.

---

## 16. Suspended Status

A Circle may be suspended when its operations must be temporarily restricted.

Possible reasons include:

- Serious compliance failure
- Fraud
- Misconduct
- Governance breakdown
- Operational failure
- Platform risk
- Ongoing investigation
- Serious reputation or member-safety concerns

A suspension record should include:

- Reason
- Effective date
- Decision authority
- Scope of restrictions
- Review date
- Reinstatement conditions
- Supporting evidence
- Audit trail

The exact restrictions during suspension are not finalised in this Founder Decision.

---

## 17. Merged Status

A Circle enters Merged status when it is formally combined with another Circle.

The old Circle record must remain preserved.

Merged status must not delete:

- Member history
- Seat history
- Governance history
- Meeting history
- Referral history
- Performance history
- Financial references
- Audit records

---

## 18. Archived Status

A Circle enters Archived status when it is no longer operational but its records remain preserved.

Archived is not the same as deleted.

An Archived Circle may remain available to authorised roles for:

- Historical reporting
- Compliance review
- Financial reference
- Audit
- Legal or operational review

---

## 19. Founding-Member Requirement

A Circle does not need all 40 members before activation.

The approved minimum founding-member requirement is:

> **15 founding members**

The approved membership stages are:

```text
0–14 members
Formation Stage

15–39 members
Active Growth Stage

40 members
Full Capacity
```

The Circle may continue recruiting after activation until it reaches 40 members.

---

## 20. Founding-Member Recruitment

The Connect BDP recruits and proposes the founding members.

The platform must verify applicable requirements, including:

- Identity
- Business legitimacy
- KYC
- Membership eligibility
- Category eligibility
- Business specialization
- Seat availability
- Specialization conflicts
- Payment status where applicable
- Acceptance of terms

The Connect BDP cannot bypass verification.

The exact KYC document list is not finalised in this Founder Decision.

---

## 21. Circle Formation and Activation Are Separate

Circle formation and Circle activation are separate stages.

### Formation means:

- The creation request is approved
- Founding members are being recruited
- Verification is underway
- Seats are being reviewed
- Governance is being prepared
- The Circle is not yet fully operational

### Activation means:

- Mandatory formation requirements are completed
- The platform completes final review
- The platform formally activates the Circle
- The Circle may begin official operations

---

## 22. Provisionally Formed Circle

A Circle may be treated as provisionally formed when:

- Creation request is approved
- Minimum founding-member requirement is met
- Founding-member verification is substantially complete
- Business specializations are checked
- Seats are validated
- Initial governance arrangements are prepared
- Initial meeting is planned

Provisionally formed does not equal Active.

---

## 23. Circle Activation Conditions

Circle activation may include confirmation of:

- Minimum 15 founding members
- Founding-member eligibility
- Required verification
- Specialization-seat validity
- Governance setup
- Required Circle-role appointment or election
- Operational support-role assignment
- Initial meeting readiness
- Platform compliance approval

The exact final activation checklist is not fully approved.

Cursor and developers must not add new activation conditions without Founder approval.

---

## 24. Maximum Circle Capacity

The approved maximum physical membership capacity is:

> **40 members per Circle**

The platform must prevent:

- Over-capacity activation
- Hidden manual seats
- Duplicate active specialization seats
- Multiple active members occupying the same exclusive specialization seat
- Unapproved temporary membership above capacity

---

## 25. Business-Specialization Seat Principle

The approved hierarchy is:

```text
GCE Power Sector
        ↓
Business Specialization
        ↓
Business Tags
```

The approved exclusivity rule is:

> **One Business Specialization = One Exclusive Seat per Circle**

A Business Tag describes expertise or service focus.

A Tag does not replace the primary Business Specialization.

The same Business Specialization may exist in different Circles.

Exclusivity applies within each Circle unless a later Founder Decision changes its scope.

---

## 26. Membership and Circle Seat Separation

The approved principle is:

> **Membership belongs to the approved member account. A Circle seat belongs to a specific Business Specialization inside a specific Circle.**

Therefore, the platform must separately manage:

- Membership status
- Circle allocation
- Circle seat
- Business Specialization
- Business Tags
- Seat reservation
- Seat activation
- Seat transfer
- Seat release
- Waitlist status

An active membership does not automatically guarantee immediate Circle placement.

---

## 27. Seat Availability and Locking

Before allocating a Circle seat, the platform should perform a seat-availability check.

Approved conceptual flow:

```text
Business Specialization selected
        ↓
Platform checks seat availability
        ↓
Seat available?
      /       \
    Yes        No
     ↓          ↓
Reserve seat   Offer waitlist or another Circle
     ↓
Complete verification
     ↓
Lock specialization
     ↓
Activate Circle seat
```

The approved seat reservation period is governed by FD-022 and is:

> **7 days**

---

## 28. Circle Naming

Circle names should follow a standard platform-controlled naming structure.

Recommended format:

```text
[Geography] + Circle + [Sequence Number]
```

Examples:

```text
Delhi South Circle 01
Delhi South Circle 02
Ludhiana Central Circle 01
```

The naming system should support:

- Search
- Reporting
- Geographic identification
- Sequential expansion
- Duplicate prevention
- Clear platform administration

Members, Connect BDPs, or Circle Boards must not create unrestricted informal names as the primary system name.

A future display-name feature is not approved in this Founder Decision.

---

## 29. Multiple Circles in the Same Territory

More than one Circle may exist in the same territory.

A new Circle may begin formation when an existing Circle reaches approximately:

> **80% occupancy**

For a 40-member Circle, 80% occupancy is:

> **32 active members**

This rule allows expansion before the existing Circle becomes fully occupied.

---

## 30. New Circle Independence

Every additional Circle is a separate operational entity.

It must have its own:

- Circle ID
- Members
- Specialization seats
- Governance
- Meetings
- Referral records
- Performance records
- Lifecycle status
- Health Score
- Audit history

A new Circle is not merely an informal extension of an existing Circle.

---

## 31. Circle Expansion

Circle expansion may be approved when:

- Existing Circle occupancy reaches approximately 32 members
- Business demand justifies another Circle
- Suitable founding members exist
- Operational capacity exists
- Territory rules permit expansion
- Platform validation is completed

The Connect BDP must submit a separate Circle creation request.

---

## 32. Circle Merger

Two Circles may be merged when necessary.

Possible reasons include:

- Low active membership
- Persistent operational weakness
- Territory restructuring
- Business viability concerns
- Governance failure
- Strategic restructuring approved by GCE

A merger must preserve the complete history of both Circles.

---

## 33. Merger Controls

Before a merger is completed, the platform must review:

- Member transfer
- Duplicate Business Specializations
- Seat conflicts
- Geography
- Governance restructuring
- Connect BDP responsibility
- Member communication
- Effective date
- Surviving or new Circle identity

Members must not be transferred automatically without validation.

The exact merger voting requirement, notice period, and approval workflow are not yet Founder Approved.

---

## 34. Circle Split

A Circle may be split, or used as the basis for creating another Circle, when growth or restructuring requires it.

A split must consider:

- Member consent or approved transfer policy
- Business-Specialization balance
- Seat conflicts
- Geography
- Governance viability
- Operational viability
- Connect BDP responsibility
- Formation requirements for the new Circle

A split creates separate operational Circle records.

The original history must remain preserved.

---

## 35. Split Controls

A Circle must not be divided arbitrarily.

The platform should validate:

- Whether both resulting Circles are viable
- Whether founding-member requirements are met
- Whether seat conflicts are resolved
- Whether governance can be established
- Whether member transfers are permitted
- Whether the territory supports the split

The exact voting, notice, and consent rules remain unresolved.

---

## 36. Circle Closure

A Circle may stop operating because of:

- Long-term inactivity
- Insufficient active membership
- Governance failure
- Serious compliance failure
- Fraud
- Misconduct
- Territory restructuring
- Connect BDP franchise cancellation
- Platform-approved strategic closure

Circle closure must result in an approved lifecycle state, such as:

- Suspended
- Merged
- Archived

Circle closure must never result in silent deletion.

---

## 37. Connect BDP Cancellation or Reassignment

If the responsible Connect BDP resigns, is suspended, or is terminated:

- The Circle remains a GCE asset
- The Circle must not be deleted
- Member access and history must be protected
- A new Connect BDP or authorised platform manager may be assigned
- Historical BDP attribution must remain preserved
- New authority begins from the approved reassignment date

Commercial and commission effects are governed by the applicable Connect BDP Founder Decision.

---

## 38. Circle Governance

Every active Circle should have an approved governance structure.

The governance structure may include:

- Chairperson
- Vice Chairperson
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Membership Coordinator
- Referral Coordinator
- Events Coordinator
- Compliance Coordinator
- Sergeant-at-Arms
- Other approved support roles

The Board governs approved internal Circle matters.

The Board does not own the Circle.

---

## 39. Board Authority Limits

The Board of Governance must not:

- Create a new Circle independently
- Activate a Circle
- Own the Circle
- Override GCE decisions
- Change official platform taxonomy directly
- Move platform funds without explicit authority
- Grant unrestricted platform permissions
- Delete Circle history
- Exceed the 40-member capacity

---

## 40. Board Eligibility

Approved eligibility principles include:

- Minimum 6 months of membership
- Minimum 75% attendance
- No active disciplinary issue
- No overdue dues
- Required trust or performance standing
- Compliance with GCE rules

Exact Trust Rank or performance thresholds are not finalised unless defined in another approved Founder Decision.

---

## 41. Board Tenure

The approved Board tenure is:

- One-year term
- Maximum two consecutive terms
- One-year cooling period after two consecutive terms

A change to this rule requires another Founder Decision.

---

## 42. Board Elections

Approved election principles include:

- Secret digital voting
- One eligible member, one vote
- Highest valid votes wins
- Auditable election records
- Tie-breaking through approved criteria
- Runoff where required

Approved tie-breaking order may include:

1. Trust Rank
2. Attendance
3. Referral performance
4. Runoff vote

The exact technical election workflow belongs in a governance workflow document.

---

## 43. Board Removal and No-Confidence

Approved no-confidence principles include:

- Petition supported by at least 25% of eligible members
- Validation by the authorised platform relationship role
- Removal requiring at least 60% of valid votes
- Interim arrangement where needed
- New election within 30 days

GCE may also remove a governance officer for:

- Serious misconduct
- Fraud
- Compliance failure
- Conflict of interest
- Material platform risk

---

## 44. Board Vacancies

When a Board position becomes vacant:

- An interim arrangement may be approved
- A replacement election may be scheduled
- The process must be auditable
- One member should not hold conflicting Board roles without approval

The exact vacancy timeline is not finalised here.

---

## 45. Board Meetings and Reporting

Approved principles include:

- Board meeting at least once per month
- Quarterly governance reporting
- Quarterly Circle review
- Conflict-of-interest disclosure
- Preservation of governance records
- Auditable decisions

The exact report format is not finalised.

---

## 46. Sergeant-at-Arms

The Sergeant-at-Arms supports disciplined and professional Circle meetings.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Maintaining meeting discipline
- Ensuring punctuality
- Timing presentations
- Monitoring meeting decorum
- Coordinating seating
- Supporting attendance
- Supporting guest registration
- Assisting with emergencies
- Assisting the Board
- Maintaining professionalism

The Sergeant-at-Arms does not have unrestricted disciplinary or administrative authority.

- Circle Members access their assigned Circle

## 61. Decision Statement

GCE will manage every GCE Connect Circle as a GCE-owned, platform-approved, lifecycle-controlled business networking entity.

A Circle may be activated with 15 founding members, may grow to a maximum of 40 active members, and must always retain a defined lifecycle status and complete audit history.

Circle creation, activation, growth, governance, expansion, merger, split, suspension, and archival must follow controlled platform processes.

This decision remains active until explicitly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
