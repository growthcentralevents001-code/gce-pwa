# PART A — GCE ENTERPRISE POSITIONING

## 2. Definition

GCE Enterprise is:

> A technology-enabled, stakeholder-fulfilled corporate event procurement, coordination, matching, workflow, and financial-control platform.

GCE Enterprise serves:

- Companies
- Institutions
- Associations
- Corporate groups
- Government or semi-government entities where approved
- Educational institutions
- Business networks
- Other verified enterprise clients

## 3. No Direct Physical Execution by GCE

GCE does not directly perform physical event execution.

GCE does not itself become the:

- Venue operator
- Caterer
- Production vendor
- Decorator
- AV vendor
- Transport provider
- Security provider
- Hospitality provider
- Event manpower provider
- Physical event-management contractor

Physical fulfilment is performed by approved third-party stakeholders and vendors.

## 4. GCE Platform Role

GCE is responsible for the digital and governance layer, including:

- Client onboarding
- Requirement capture
- Project creation
- Enterprise BDP attribution
- Enterprise Platform Expert assignment
- Service requirement breakdown
- Vendor discovery
- Vendor verification framework
- Vendor shortlisting
- Quotation workflows
- Commercial comparison
- Client approval workflows
- Work-order workflows
- Payment tracking
- Settlement controls
- Commission calculations
- Vendor Opportunity Fee tracking
- Readiness tracking
- Completion evidence
- Ratings
- Dispute escalation
- Audit trails
- Notifications
- Dashboard access
- Platform records

## 5. Fulfilment Providers

Enterprise requirements may be fulfilled by:

- Approved GCE Connect Circle members
- Approved Venue Partners
- Approved Marketplace participants
- Approved service vendors
- Verified external vendors where no suitable GCE stakeholder is available
- Other approved GCE ecosystem participants

The selected vendor remains responsible for physical delivery.

# PART C — ENTERPRISE CLIENT ARCHITECTURE

## 8. Enterprise Client

An Enterprise Client is a verified organisation or approved enterprise account using GCE Enterprise for event procurement and coordination.

The exact KYB and legal-document requirements remain Pending Legal and Compliance Review.

## 9. Client Onboarding

Client onboarding may require:

- Organisation identity
- Authorised representative
- Billing details
- GST details where applicable
- Contracting entity
- Event requirement
- Budget range
- City or cities
- Event date or date range
- Expected attendance
- Service requirements
- Approval authority
- Contract signatory
- Payment contact

## 10. Client-Based Allocation

Enterprise BDP allocation is:

> **Client-based, not territory-based**

An Enterprise BDP may source clients from any city, subject to:

- Valid attribution
- No duplicate assignment
- Platform approval
- Client consent
- Conflict handling
- Active-client capacity

No Enterprise BDP receives permanent geographic ownership.

## 11. Active Client Definition

A client counts toward the active-client cap only when:

- The organisation is verified
- An authorised representative is identified
- The client is formally attributed to the Enterprise BDP
- A qualified commercial relationship exists
- There is an open opportunity, active proposal, live project, recently completed protected project, or another approved active relationship state

A raw lead, unverified contact, or business card does not automatically consume an active-client slot.

## 12. Recommended Client Statuses

Operational statuses may include:

- Prospect
- Qualified
- Pending Verification
- Attributed
- Requirement Discovery
- Proposal Active
- Contracted
- Project Active
- Completed — Protected
- Dormant
- Released
- Disputed
- Blocked

Exact technical enum names remain Pending Technical Design.

## 13. Client Capacity

One active Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack provides capacity for:

> **Up to 30 active Enterprise Clients**

The cap exists to:

- Prevent client hoarding
- Protect service quality
- Improve response time
- Maintain manageable workload
- Support clear attribution
- Enable scalable commercial expansion

## 14. Additional Client Capacity

To manage more than 30 active Enterprise Clients, the Enterprise BDP must purchase and activate:

> **An additional Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack**

Each additional pack provides capacity for up to 30 additional active clients.

## 15. Standard Pack Limit

The standard limit is:

> **Maximum two active Enterprise BDP Franchise Packs per individual or controlled business entity**

This means a standard maximum of:

> **60 active Enterprise Clients**

A higher limit requires special platform approval based on:

- Performance
- Team capacity
- Client satisfaction
- Compliance
- Response time
- Revenue quality
- Platform strategy

# PART D — ENTERPRISE BDP

## 16. Official Role Name

The approved role name is:

> **GCE Enterprise Business Development Partner**

Approved short name:

> **Enterprise BDP**

## 17. Enterprise BDP Commercial Status

An Enterprise BDP is:

> An independent GCE business partner authorised to acquire, onboard, and manage attributed Enterprise Client relationships through an approved Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack.

The Enterprise BDP is not automatically:

- A GCE employee
- A platform administrator
- An owner of clients
- An owner of projects
- An owner of vendor relationships
- An owner of platform data
- An event executor
- A legal signatory for GCE
- An approver of personal commission
- An approver of vendor settlement

## 18. Enterprise BDP Responsibilities

The Enterprise BDP may be responsible for:

- Lead generation
- Client qualification
- Client onboarding support
- Initial requirement capture
- Requirement-brief coordination
- Client communication
- Proposal presentation
- Commercial coordination
- Contract follow-up
- Purchase-order follow-up
- Payment follow-up
- Client relationship management
- Repeat-business development
- Post-project feedback
- Accurate platform records
- Coordination with the Enterprise Platform Expert

## 19. Enterprise BDP Authority Limits

The Enterprise BDP must not independently:

- Approve quotations
- Change platform commission
- Offer unauthorised discounts
- Sign contracts for GCE
- Select vendors finally
- Approve vendor payments
- Approve refunds
- Approve settlements
- Approve personal commission

- Promise guaranteed income

- Access unrelated client or vendor data

# PART E — ENTERPRISE BDP FRANCHISE PACK

## 20. Franchise Pack Definition

An Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack is a platform-approved commercial and operating capacity that provides:

- Enterprise BDP certification
- Training
- Dashboard access
- Client attribution capacity
- Opportunity-management tools
- Commission tracking
- Up to 30 active-client capacity

Each Franchise Pack must have its own:

- Pack ID
- Assigned Enterprise BDP
- Activation status
- Payment status
- Active-client capacity
- Performance target
- Commission records
- Finance-recovery records where applicable
- Audit history

## 21. Direct-Payment Package

The approved direct-payment package is:

> **₹30,000 upfront per Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack**

The fee is:

- One-time
- Non-refundable after training or activation
- Payable separately for every additional pack
- Not a security deposit
- Not a purchase of client ownership
- Not a purchase of permanent territory
- Not a guarantee of income

## 22. Package Inclusions

The Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack may include:

- Enterprise BDP certification
- Corporate sales training
- Requirement-discovery training
- Proposal-presentation training
- Commercial-policy training
- Contract and purchase-order workflow training
- Client relationship-management training
- Enterprise dashboard access
- Client and opportunity-management tools
- Compliance and data-protection training
- Vendor coordination overview
- Ongoing platform support according to policy

## 23. No Launch Discount

There is no launch-phase discounted Franchise Pack fee.

The direct-payment fee remains:

> **₹30,000**

Any future discount requires separate Founder approval.

## 24. Financed Package

The approved financed package is:

> **₹36,000 total**

Structure:

- Initial payment: **₹5,000**
- Recoverable balance: **₹31,000**
- Maximum monthly recovery: **Up to ₹5,000**
- Recovery source: **Earned and approved Enterprise BDP commission only**

## 25. Finance Recovery Rules

The finance-recovery model must follow these rules:

- Recovery occurs only from earned and approved commission
- GCE recovers the lower of ₹5,000 or the approved commission available for that month
- No automatic cash-shortfall demand arises when commission is below ₹5,000
- Unrecovered balance carries forward
- No additional interest accrues beyond the fixed ₹36,000 package value
- Every deduction must be visible in the dashboard and financial ledger
- Exit or suspension does not automatically erase the outstanding balance
- Any recovery after exit must follow the signed agreement

## 26. Separate Pack Requirement

Every additional 30-client capacity requires:

- Separate approval
- Separate Franchise Pack
- Separate ₹30,000 upfront package; or
- Separate ₹36,000 financed package
- Separate performance target
- Separate capacity tracking
- Separate audit trail

# PART F — ENTERPRISE BDP PERFORMANCE

## 27. Monthly Revenue Target

Each active Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack has a target of:

> **₹3,00,000 eligible Enterprise event revenue per month**

The target applies separately to each active pack.

## 28. Rolling Three-Month Benchmark

The stronger performance benchmark is:

> **₹9,00,000 eligible Enterprise event revenue over a rolling three-month period per Franchise Pack**

This reflects the longer and uneven sales cycle of Enterprise projects.

## 29. Eligible Revenue for Target Measurement

Target revenue must be:

- Successfully collected
- Linked to valid Enterprise projects
- Correctly attributed
- Excluding GST and statutory taxes
- Excluding refunds
- Excluding reversals
- Excluding chargebacks
- Excluding cancelled projects
- Excluding uncollected invoices

Proposal value, verbal commitment, and uncollected purchase orders do not count as achieved revenue.

## 30. Performance Review Trigger

Formal performance review may be triggered when:

- The monthly target is missed for two consecutive months; or
- The rolling three-month target is missed; or
- Client servicing falls materially below policy

## 31. Corrective Process

The approved progressive process is:

1. Formal performance review
2. Written corrective plan
3. Sixty-day improvement period
4. Additional training or supervision
5. Restriction on onboarding new clients
6. Reduction or reassignment of active-client capacity
7. Suspension or cancellation after persistent failure

Serious misconduct may bypass this sequence.

## 32. Quality Metrics

Enterprise BDP performance may also consider:

- Qualified clients
- Active opportunities
- Proposal conversion
- Collection rate
- Client response time
- Client satisfaction
- Repeat projects
- Project handover quality
- Complaint levels
- Data accuracy
- Compliance
- Misrepresentation incidents

The exact performance score formula remains unresolved.

# PART G — ENTERPRISE PLATFORM EXPERT

## 33. Role Definition

The approved role name is:

> **GCE Enterprise Platform Expert**

Short name:

> **Enterprise Platform Expert**

The Enterprise Platform Expert is an internal GCE employee or directly controlled specialist.

The role is not an independent franchise role.

## 34. Controlled-Access Requirement

Because the Enterprise Platform Expert may access sensitive platform data, the role must operate under:

- GCE employment; or
- A directly controlled contractual arrangement
- Confidentiality obligations
- Data-protection obligations
- Role-based access
- Project-scoped access
- Audit monitoring
- Conflict-of-interest disclosure
- Immediate access-revocation capability

A directly contracted specialist must be subject to controls equivalent to an authorised internal user.

## 35. Enterprise Platform Expert Responsibilities

The Enterprise Platform Expert may:

- Review the Event Requirement Brief
- Break the event into service requirements
- Search GCE platform data
- Identify relevant Connect Circle members
- Identify Venue Partners
- Identify Marketplace participants
- Identify verified vendors
- Prepare vendor shortlists
- Invite quotations
- Compare quotations
- Prepare consolidated proposal inputs
- Coordinate approval workflows
- Track vendor readiness
- Track completion evidence
- Escalate service issues
- Coordinate digital project closure
- Maintain project records

## 36. Enterprise Platform Expert Restrictions

The Enterprise Platform Expert must not:

- Physically execute the event
- Collect money personally
- Approve personal compensation
- Manipulate quotations
- Hide competing vendors
- Add unverified vendors without approval
- Change client pricing without authority
- Sign contracts for GCE
- Approve refunds independently
- Approve settlements independently
- Accept vendor kickbacks
- Access unrelated project data
- Guarantee vendor performance

## 37. Compensation Principle

The Enterprise Platform Expert may receive:

- Fixed salary
- Fixed retainer
- Project-based incentive
- Approved performance bonus

The exact salary, retainer, project incentive, or bonus amount is not locked by this Founder Decision.

It remains subject to:

- HR policy
- Finance approval
- Employment or contract terms
- Founder approval where required

No fixed percentage of Vendor Opportunity Fees is approved at this stage.

## 38. Expert Capacity

The recommended launch capacity is:

> **Maximum 10 active standard Enterprise projects per Enterprise Platform Expert**

A major multi-city project may require a lower practical workload limit or multiple experts.

Exact weighted-capacity rules remain Pending Technical and Operating Design.

# PART K — COMMERCIAL ARCHITECTURE

## 52. Standard GCE Platform Commission

The standard GCE Enterprise platform commission is:

> **20% of eligible Enterprise event revenue**

## 53. Reduced Commission Range

A reduced platform commission between:

> **15% and 19%**

may be approved for qualifying strategic projects.

Examples may include:

- Large projects
- Repeat clients
- Annual contracts
- Multi-city engagements
- Competitive tenders
- Strategic institutional clients

A reduced rate is not automatically available.

Below 15% requires special Founder or senior-authority approval.

## 54. Eligible Enterprise Event Revenue

Eligible Enterprise event revenue means approved event value successfully collected from the Enterprise Client, excluding:

- GST
- Other statutory taxes
- Refunds
- Reversals
- Chargebacks
- Failed payments
- Complimentary services
- Unapproved pass-through amounts
- Fraudulent transactions
- Amounts not actually received

## 55. Enterprise BDP Commission

The Enterprise BDP earns:

> **25% of the eligible platform commission actually earned by GCE from the attributed Enterprise project**

There is no tiered Enterprise BDP commission at launch.

## 56. Commission Formula

```text
Eligible Enterprise Event Revenue
× Approved GCE Platform Commission Rate
= GCE Platform Commission

GCE Platform Commission
× 25%
= Enterprise BDP Commission
```

## 57. Commission Example at 20%

```text
Eligible Event Revenue: ₹10,00,000
GCE Platform Commission at 20%: ₹2,00,000
Enterprise BDP Commission at 25%: ₹50,000
GCE Retained Commission: ₹1,50,000
```

The effective Enterprise BDP earning is 5% of eligible event revenue at a 20% platform commission.

## 58. Commission Example at 15%

```text
Eligible Event Revenue: ₹10,00,000
GCE Platform Commission at 15%: ₹1,50,000
Enterprise BDP Commission at 25%: ₹37,500
GCE Retained Commission: ₹1,12,500
```

The effective Enterprise BDP earning is 3.75% of eligible event revenue at a 15% platform commission.

## 59. Commission at Monthly Target

At ₹3,00,000 eligible event revenue:

| Platform Rate | GCE Platform Commission | BDP Share at 25% | GCE Retained Share |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20% | ₹60,000 | ₹15,000 | ₹45,000 |
| 15% | ₹45,000 | ₹11,250 | ₹33,750 |

These are illustrative examples only.

They are not guaranteed income.

## 60. Commission Earning Trigger

Enterprise BDP commission becomes earned proportionately only after:

- Corresponding client payment is successfully collected
- Related project milestone is approved
- Platform commission becomes settlement eligible
- Attribution is valid
- No material refund, dispute, or chargeback exists

## 61. Commission Payout

Commission may be released proportionately according to:

- Client payment milestones
- Settlement eligibility
- Approved reconciliation
- Refund and chargeback status
- Finance-recovery deductions where applicable

Exact payout dates remain subject to FD-021 and Pending Technical Design.

## 62. Repeat-Project Commission

The Enterprise BDP continues receiving 25% of eligible platform commission on repeat projects from an attributed Enterprise Client while:

- The Enterprise BDP remains active
- The client remains attributed
- The BDP remains materially involved
- The client relationship remains protected
- The project is eligible

# PART L — CLIENT ATTRIBUTION, DORMANCY, AND REASSIGNMENT

## 63. Attribution Protection

An Enterprise Client remains attributed to the Enterprise BDP:

- While an active opportunity exists
- While a project is active
- During the approved repeat-client protection period

The recommended protection period is:

> **12 months from the most recent successfully completed attributed project**

## 64. Dormant-Client Review

A client with no qualified opportunity, documented client activity, proposal, project, or approved relationship activity for:

> **90 consecutive days**

may be reviewed for Dormant status.

Before release:

- Notify the BDP
- Allow evidence of engagement
- Conduct platform review
- Preserve historical attribution

A Completed — Protected client may remain protected during the 12-month repeat-project window.

## 65. Duplicate-Client Rules

Client attribution disputes should consider:

1. Existing verified attribution
2. Documented first meaningful engagement
3. Approved client introduction
4. Client confirmation
5. Platform decision

An email address alone does not establish client ownership.

Attribution evidence may include:

- Client-submitted enquiry
- Verified meeting
- Requirement brief
- Client acknowledgement
- Proposal activity
- Platform-recorded communication

No BDP may sell, trade, or privately transfer client attribution.

## 66. Reassignment

A client may be reassigned when:

- The BDP exits
- The BDP becomes inactive
- The client requests reassignment
- The BDP fails to service the client
- A dispute arises
- Fraud or misconduct occurs
- The client becomes dormant
- Attribution was incorrect
- Platform strategy requires reassignment

Historical attribution must remain preserved.

## 67. Commission After Reassignment

- Earned and approved commission remains payable subject to valid deductions
- Pending commission remains subject to eligibility review
- Future project commission follows the approved reassignment date
- Historical earned commission must not be silently transferred
- Exact treatment of projects spanning the reassignment date remains Pending Founder Approval

# PART M — PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT

## 68. Standard Payment Structure

The standard Enterprise payment structure is:

- 30% at project confirmation
- 40% at approved execution or readiness milestone
- 30% after completion

This is a standard model, not an inflexible rule.

Alternative schedules may be approved based on:

- Project size
- Vendor commitments
- Project duration
- Client credit profile
- Procurement rules
- Risk level

## 69. Payment Is Not Settlement Eligibility

Client payment does not automatically mean:

- Vendor settlement is payable
- Enterprise BDP commission is payable
- Vendor Opportunity Fee is earned
- Refund risk has ended
- Project milestone is approved

All financial movements must follow FD-020 and FD-021.

## 70. Vendor Settlement

Vendor settlement requires:

- Valid work order
- Deliverable verification
- Completion evidence
- Applicable client or platform approval
- Invoice verification
- Compliance verification
- Dispute and deduction review
- Settlement eligibility

GCE may charge a success-based:

> **Enterprise Vendor Opportunity Fee**

## 72. Separate Revenue Pool

The Vendor Opportunity Fee must remain separate from:

- Client-side GCE platform commission
- Enterprise BDP commission
- Vendor settlement principal
- Taxes
- Refunds

A Vendor Opportunity Fee should become eligible only after:

- Vendor selection
- Client approval
- Work-order acceptance
- Eligible payment collection
- Applicable milestone completion
- No material refund or dispute exists

## 74. Recommended Launch Principle

The recommended launch model is:

> **Success-based Vendor Opportunity Fee**

A fixed lead fee is not approved as the primary launch model.

## 75. Unresolved Fee Rate and Distribution

The exact Vendor Opportunity Fee percentage is not finalised.

The exact distribution of the Vendor Opportunity Fee is not finalised.

No automatic percentage is approved for:

- Enterprise BDP
- Enterprise Platform Expert
- Referral source
- Connect Circle member
- Venue Partner
- GCE Platform

## 91. Decision Statement

GCE Enterprise will operate as a technology-enabled, stakeholder-fulfilled corporate event procurement and coordination platform.

GCE will not directly execute physical events. Enterprise requirements will be sourced and managed through Enterprise BDPs, structured by internal or directly controlled Enterprise Platform Experts, and fulfilled by approved Connect Circle members, Venue Partners, Marketplace participants, and verified vendors.

Each Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack provides capacity for up to 30 active Enterprise Clients and requires either a ₹30,000 direct payment or an approved ₹36,000 financed package.

The Enterprise BDP earns a flat 25% of the eligible platform commission actually earned by GCE from attributed Enterprise projects.

The standard GCE platform commission is 20% of eligible Enterprise event revenue, with reduced rates between 15% and 19% allowed only through authorised approval.

This decision remains active until explicitly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
