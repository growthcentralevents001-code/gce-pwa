# FD-027 — Membership Commercial and Operating Architecture

**Decision ID:** FD-027
**Title:** Membership Commercial and Operating Architecture
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** GCE Connect Membership Commercial and Operating Constitution
**Authority Level:** Founder Decision
**Platform:** Growth Central Events (GCE)
**Applies To:** GCE Connect Circle Membership, Associate Tier, future Core Tier, Circle Members, Circle seats, Business Specializations, Business Tags, renewals, upgrades, freezes, transfers, refunds, rejoining, Connect BDP commission attribution, platform-only membership workflows, and related operating controls

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision defines the approved commercial and operating architecture for GCE Connect Circle Membership.

It establishes:

- The official membership naming structure
- The launch-phase membership model
- Associate Tier pricing and benefits
- Future Core Tier architecture
- Achievement-based Core eligibility
- Network-readiness requirements
- Platform-only membership workflows
- Circle seat and membership separation
- Business Specialization and Business Tag rules
- Renewal, grace, upgrade, downgrade, freeze, transfer, refund, suspension, termination, and rejoining rules
- Membership revenue attribution
- Connect BDP commission interaction
- Governance and taxonomy boundaries
- Lead Assist separation
- Unresolved items that must not be invented

This Founder Decision must be read together with:

- FD-001 — Business Model
- FD-020 — Financial and Wallet Architecture
- FD-021 — Settlement Engine
- FD-022 — Membership Lifecycle
- FD-023 — RBAC and Permissions
- FD-024 — GCE Connect Circle Lifecycle
- FD-025 — Connect BDP Commercial and Operating Architecture

# PART A — OFFICIAL MEMBERSHIP NAMING

## 2. Formal Membership Name

The approved formal launch membership name is:

> **GCE Connect Circle Membership — Associate Tier**

## 3. Member Title

A person holding an active GCE Connect Circle Membership is referred to as:

> **GCE Connect Circle Member**

The member’s tier is shown separately.

Launch tier:

> **Associate Tier**

Future upgrade tier:

> **Core Tier**

## 4. Future Core Membership Name

The approved future name is:

> **GCE Connect Circle Membership — Core Tier**

Core Tier is not available for direct purchase during launch.

## 5. Naming Hierarchy

```text
GCE Connect
└── Circle Membership
    ├── Associate Tier
    └── Core Tier
```

The person remains a GCE Connect Circle Member regardless of tier.

Associate and Core are tier labels, not separate personal identities.

---

# PART B — LAUNCH-PHASE MEMBERSHIP MODEL

## 6. Associate-Only Launch

During the launch phase, only the following membership is available for direct purchase:

> **GCE Connect Circle Membership — Associate Tier**

Core Tier is not available for direct purchase during launch.

## 7. Associate Tier Price

The approved Associate Tier subscription price is:

> **₹6,000 per quarter**

Applicable GST and statutory taxes are additional.

## 8. Billing Frequency

The approved launch billing frequency is:

> **Quarterly billing only**

Monthly and annual plans are not active at launch.

Any future monthly or annual plan requires separate Founder approval.

## 9. Platform-Only Availability

GCE Connect Circle Membership may be applied for, verified, paid for, activated, renewed, upgraded, downgraded, transferred, frozen, suspended, terminated, or rejoined only through the authorised GCE platform workflow.

No unofficial, offline, verbal, manual, or privately issued membership is valid.

## 10. Connect BDP Support Role

The Connect BDP may:

- Explain the membership
- Support business verification
- Assist with onboarding
- Recommend an appropriate Business Specialization
- Recommend appropriate Business Tags
- Support Circle seat selection
- Assist with platform usage
- Support renewal and retention

The Connect BDP may not:

- Activate membership manually
- Collect payment in a personal account
- Issue an unofficial membership
- Promise a seat without platform confirmation
- Change pricing
- Add unapproved Tags
- Bypass KYC
- Override seat exclusivity
- Guarantee Core eligibility
- Guarantee referrals or revenue

# PART C — ASSOCIATE TIER BENEFITS

## 11. Approved Associate Tier Benefits

The Associate Tier may include:

- Eligibility for one approved primary Circle seat
- One approved Business Specialization
- Two included Business Tags
- Ability to purchase up to two additional approved Tags
- Local Circle networking
- Local referrals and business opportunities
- Digital member profile
- App-based referral management
- Dual-confirmed closed-business records
- Trust and performance history
- Attendance and meeting tools
- Circle governance participation
- Member support
- Access to available platform opportunities
- Core eligibility progress tracking
- Access to approved GCE Connect activities
- Access to secure platform infrastructure
- Notifications, dashboards, and analytics according to policy

## 12. No Guaranteed Business Outcome

Membership does not guarantee referrals, leads, revenue, closed business, client acquisition, nationwide opportunity, Core upgrade, permanent Circle seat, lifetime category protection, or any fixed return on subscription.

Membership provides access to the GCE digital business-growth ecosystem and its approved services.

# PART D — MEMBERSHIP AND CIRCLE SEAT SEPARATION

## 13. Separate Membership and Seat

A paid membership does not automatically guarantee a particular Circle seat.

A member requires both:

1. An active eligible membership
2. A separately approved Circle seat

## 14. Seat Eligibility

Circle seat approval remains subject to:

- User and business verification
- KYC or required compliance checks
- Business eligibility
- Business Specialization availability
- Circle capacity
- Taxonomy compatibility
- Platform approval
- Governance and conduct requirements

## 15. One Primary Circle Seat

One active membership provides eligibility for:

> **One primary Circle seat**

A member does not receive multiple physical seats under one membership.

## 16. One Member, One Seat

The approved principle is:

> **One member = one physical Circle seat**

Multiple Business Tags do not create additional physical seats, introductions, voting rights, member identities, or Circle ownership.

---

# PART E — BUSINESS SPECIALIZATION AND TAGS

## 17. Business Specialization

Each active Circle Member may hold:

> **One approved Business Specialization seat within the Circle**

Business Specialization defines the member’s protected primary business seat.

## 18. Maximum Business Tags

A member may hold:

> **Maximum four approved Business Tags**

## 19. Included Tags

The Associate Tier includes:

- Tag 1 — Included
- Tag 2 — Included

## 20. Paid Tags

Additional approved Tags are charged as follows:

- Tag 3 — **+25% of the active base subscription**
- Tag 4 — **+25% of the active base subscription**

The fourth Tag is not charged at +50%.

Any old +50% reference is obsolete.

## 21. Associate Tier Tag Pricing Example

For an Associate Tier base subscription of ₹6,000 per quarter:

| Tag | Charge |
|---|---:|
| Tag 1 | Included |
| Tag 2 | Included |
| Tag 3 | ₹1,500 per quarter |
| Tag 4 | ₹1,500 per quarter |

Maximum quarterly membership amount before taxes:

> **₹9,000**

## 22. Future Core Tier Tag Pricing Example

For a future Core Tier base subscription of ₹9,000 per quarter:

| Tag | Charge |
|---|---:|
| Tag 1 | Included |
| Tag 2 | Included |
| Tag 3 | ₹2,250 per quarter |
| Tag 4 | ₹2,250 per quarter |

## 23. Tag Billing

A paid Tag should:

- Follow the active membership term
- Be charged proportionately when added mid-cycle
- Renew with the membership
- Be removed at renewal if not renewed
- Require taxonomy and eligibility validation
- Not bypass Business Specialization exclusivity

The exact daily proration formula remains Pending Technical Design.

## 24. Protected Tag Scope

An approved Tag may receive protection within the relevant Circle according to platform rules.

Use the term:

> **Protected Tag Scope**

Do not use the term “business monopoly” as a constitutional label.

## 25. Vacant Tag Availability

A Tag not purchased or assigned remains available to another eligible, non-competing business, subject to taxonomy compatibility, specialization conflict review, Circle rules, and platform approval.

## 26. Tag Does Not Create Additional Seat

A third or fourth Tag expands the approved service scope of the same member.

It does not create a new physical seat, a second Business Specialization, an additional Circle Member, or additional voting authority.

# PART F — TAXONOMY GOVERNANCE

## 27. Approved Taxonomy Structure

Membership should operate through:

- GC Power Sector
- Business Specialization
- Business Tags
- Digital Member Profile
- Intelligent Referral Routing

Use:

> **GC Power Sector**

Do not use the outdated term:

> Power Circle

## 28. Local Recommendation, Platform Decision

The Circle Board or authorised local governance body may recommend a new Business Specialization, new Business Tag, merge, split, edit, or removal.

The final decision remains with the Platform Taxonomy Team.

## 29. Taxonomy Review

Taxonomy review should consider duplicate category risk, referral conflict, market demand, national scalability, local relevance, revenue opportunity, database integrity, compliance, and non-overlap.

## 30. Regional Extension

Regional or district-specific Tags may be approved where justified.

Local taxonomy changes do not become valid until approved and published by the platform.

# PART G — REFERRAL AND CLOSED-BUSINESS RULES

## 31. Platform-Recorded Referrals

All qualifying referral activity must be recorded through the GCE platform.

Verbal, meeting-only, WhatsApp-only, or off-platform referrals do not count for official referral records, performance, Trust, Core eligibility, leaderboards, commission, or reward calculations.

## 32. Referral Routing

Referral routing may use:

- GC Power Sector
- Business Specialization
- Business Tag
- Member availability
- Capacity
- Location
- Trust and performance data

Exact algorithmic rules remain subject to technical design and separate Founder approval where required.

## 33. Dual Confirmation

Closed business requires dual confirmation by:

- The lead giver
- The receiving member

Revenue is not considered verified until both required parties confirm through the platform.

## 34. Invalid Activity

The following must not count toward membership performance or Core eligibility:

- Self-referrals
- Duplicate referrals
- Fake referrals
- Cancelled business
- Reversed transactions
- Unconfirmed business
- Circular point manipulation
- Fake attendance
- Off-platform claims
- Activity under investigation
- Fraudulent transactions

# PART H — FUTURE CORE TIER

## 35. Core Tier Is Achievement-Based

Core Tier is not available for direct purchase during launch.

Core Tier may be activated only after:

- Member eligibility is achieved
- GCE network readiness is achieved
- Platform review is completed
- Upgrade payment is completed

## 36. Core Eligibility Is Earned

A member cannot directly buy Core status without qualifying.

The approved principle is:

> **Eligibility is earned; Core activation is purchased only after eligibility and platform readiness are confirmed.**

## 37. Minimum Associate Tenure

The recommended minimum tenure before Core eligibility review is:

> **Six months of continuous active Associate Tier membership**

This normally represents two completed quarterly billing cycles.

## 38. Mandatory Core Eligibility Conditions

A member should satisfy all mandatory conditions, including:

- Active Associate Tier membership for at least six months
- Valid business verification
- Required KYC or compliance status
- No unresolved serious compliance case
- No unresolved disciplinary case
- Fully paid membership dues
- Required profile completeness
- Referral activity recorded through the platform
- No detected manipulation or fraud
- Satisfactory Circle participation

## 39. Achievement Conditions

Core eligibility may consider approved achievements such as attendance, qualified referrals given, qualified referrals accepted, dual-confirmed closed business, Trust Rank, timely response to leads, positive feedback, platform usage, Circle participation, governance contribution, profile completeness, and complaint history.

The recommended launch rule is:

> All mandatory conditions plus at least four approved achievement conditions.

Exact numerical thresholds remain Pending Founder Approval.

## 40. No Complex Score at Launch

A complex weighted Core score is not approved at launch.

The platform should initially use a transparent eligibility checklist.

Any future weighted formula requires separate Founder approval.

## 41. Core Eligibility Workflow

```text
Associate Active
→ Core Progress Started
→ Achievement Conditions Completed
→ Automated Eligibility Check
→ Circle and Compliance Review
→ Network Readiness Check
→ Core Eligible
→ Core Upgrade Offered
→ Member Acceptance
→ Upgrade Payment
→ Core Active
```

Exact technical status values remain Pending Technical Design.

## 42. Core Upgrade Is Not Automatic

Completing achievements does not automatically activate Core Tier.

Core activation also requires platform approval, network readiness, no compliance hold, member acceptance, and successful payment.

## 43. Future Core Tier Price

The approved future Core Tier price is:

> **₹9,000 per quarter**

Applicable taxes are additional.

Core Tier remains unavailable for direct purchase during launch.

## 44. Mid-Cycle Core Upgrade

When an eligible Associate Tier member accepts a Core upgrade during an active quarter:

- The member pays the prorated difference
- Existing membership expiry date remains unchanged
- Core benefits activate after approval and payment
- No additional Circle seat is created

Full-quarter price difference:

```text
₹9,000 Core Tier
− ₹6,000 Associate Tier
= ₹3,000
```

Exact proration remains Pending Technical Design.

# PART L — CORE RETENTION AND REVERSION

## 53. Core Status Is Reviewable

Core status is not permanent for life.

It remains active while the Core subscription remains active, compliance remains satisfactory, business verification remains valid, minimum participation standards are maintained, and no serious manipulation or fraud occurs.

## 54. Core Review

A single weak quarter should not automatically cause reversion.

The recommended process is:

1. Warning
2. Review
3. Improvement period
4. Reversion at renewal after persistent failure
5. Immediate suspension for serious misconduct

## 55. Core Reversion

Core may revert to Associate Tier when:

- Member chooses not to renew Core
- Core payment is not completed
- Eligibility is persistently not maintained
- Member requests downgrade
- Serious compliance failure occurs
- Platform access is lawfully restricted

Reversion normally takes effect at renewal, except where immediate suspension is justified.

Historical Trust and performance records remain preserved.

# PART M — MEMBERSHIP ACTIVATION

## 56. Activation Conditions

Membership becomes active only when:

- User account is verified
- Required KYC or business verification is complete
- Plan is selected

- Membership terms are accepted
- No compliance hold exists

# PART N — SEAT RESERVATION

## 57. Seat Reservation Period

An approved Circle seat may be reserved for:

> **Seven days**

## 58. Reservation Rules

- Reservation begins after seat eligibility approval
- Payment and activation must be completed within the reservation period
- Seat is released after seven days if activation remains incomplete
- Extension requires authorised approval
- Indefinite manual holds are not allowed

---

# PART O — RENEWAL AND GRACE

## 59. Renewal Notice

Renewal notice begins:

> **Thirty days before membership expiry**

## 60. Grace Period

The approved grace period is:

> **Thirty days after expiry**

## 61. Grace-Period Treatment

During grace:

- Renewal eligibility remains
- Circle seat remains protected
- Full access may be restricted
- New Tags may not be added
- New benefits may not be activated
- Commissionable or financial activity may be restricted according to policy
- Full access restores after successful renewal

## 62. After Grace Expiry

After the grace period:

- Membership becomes inactive
- Circle seat may be released
- Waitlisted eligible applicant may receive the seat
- Historical membership data remains preserved
- Rejoining may be required

## 63. Renewal Pricing

Renewal is charged at the plan price applicable on the renewal date.

Original joining price is not permanently guaranteed.

Members should receive advance notice of material pricing changes.

---

# PART P — UPGRADE AND DOWNGRADE

## 64. Upgrade

Associate-to-Core upgrade:

- Requires Core eligibility
- Requires network readiness
- Requires platform approval
- Requires successful payment
- May use prorated price difference
- Does not create an additional Circle seat
- Does not reset membership history

## 65. Downgrade

Core-to-Associate downgrade:

- May be requested during the active term
- Normally takes effect at next renewal
- Does not create a mid-cycle refund
- Recalculates paid Tag pricing at renewal
- Preserves history and Trust records
- Does not guarantee continued access to Core-only features

---

# PART Q — FREEZE

## 66. Membership Freeze

Membership may be frozen for up to:

> **Ninety days**

subject to approval.

## 67. Freeze Conditions

- Freeze requires an approved reason
- Only one active freeze may exist at a time
- Membership benefits pause during freeze
- Membership expiry may be extended by the approved freeze period
- Referrals, meetings, and opportunity tools may be paused
- Rankings and activity metrics may be paused
- The member’s history remains preserved

## 68. Circle Seat Protection During Freeze

Recommended seat protection during freeze:

> **Up to 30 days**

For freeze periods beyond 30 days:

- Seat may be released where business continuity requires
- Member may rejoin through transfer or waitlist
- Historical membership and performance remain preserved
- No permanent seat ownership is created

## 69. Transfer Types

Membership may support:

- City transfer

- Business Tag change

## 70. Transfer Conditions

Historical membership, Trust, performance, and prior seat data must remain preserved.

# PART T — CANCELLATION AND REFUND

## 74. Before Activation

A refund may be considered when:

- GCE rejects the application
- Approved seat cannot be provided
- Platform cannot activate the service
- Duplicate payment occurs
- Billing error occurs

Applicable payment-gateway or administrative deductions may be retained where legally permitted.

## 75. After Activation

Membership fees are normally:

> **Non-refundable after activation**

## 76. No-Refund Circumstances

No refund is due merely because:

- Member does not attend meetings
- Member does not receive expected referrals
- Member does not use the platform
- Member changes their mind
- Member does not achieve Core eligibility
- Member does not receive nationwide opportunities
- Member is suspended for misconduct
- Business outcome is lower than expected

## 77. Exceptional Refunds

Exceptional refunds may require authorised approval for duplicate payment, platform billing error, legal requirement, material platform failure, or other verified exceptional circumstances.

Exact refund matrix remains Pending Founder and Legal Approval.

## 94. Decision Statement

GCE will launch GCE Connect membership through the:

> **GCE Connect Circle Membership — Associate Tier**

The person holding this membership is a:

> **GCE Connect Circle Member**

Associate Tier will be the only membership directly available for purchase during launch at ₹6,000 per quarter plus applicable taxes.

Core Tier will be an achievement-based future upgrade and will not be available for direct purchase during launch. Core activation will require verified member eligibility, adequate GCE network readiness, platform review, member acceptance, and payment of the applicable upgrade amount.

Each membership provides eligibility for one primary Circle seat, one Business Specialization, two included Business Tags, and up to two additional paid Tags. The third and fourth Tags are each charged at +25% of the active base subscription and do not create additional physical seats.

Membership operations must be completed through the authorised GCE platform. GCE does not guarantee referrals, revenue, nationwide opportunities, or Core activation.

This decision remains active until explicitly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
