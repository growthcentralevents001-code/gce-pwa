# FD-028 — Revenue Recognition and Commercial Architecture

**Decision ID:** FD-028
**Title:** Revenue Recognition and Commercial Architecture
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** Revenue Constitution and Commercial Recognition Framework
**Authority Level:** Founder Decision
**Platform:** Growth Central Events (GCE)
**Applies To:** GCE Connect, GCE Marketplace, GCE Enterprise, memberships, Business Tags, events, sponsorships, advertisements, premium listings, franchise and partner fees, administrative fees, future Lead Assist revenue, future Vendor Opportunity Fees, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, taxes, wallets, commissions, settlements, reporting, audit, and multi-currency operations

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision defines the approved revenue-recognition and commercial architecture for the GCE ecosystem.

It establishes:

- The distinction between transaction value, collected money, eligible revenue, platform revenue, and settlement-eligible amounts
- The conditions under which revenue may be operationally recognised
- The approved and future revenue categories of GCE Connect, GCE Marketplace, and GCE Enterprise
- The treatment of memberships, Tags, events, sponsorships, advertisements, premium listings, franchise fees, administrative fees, refunds, reversals, credits, taxes, deposits, and commissions
- Marketplace and Enterprise commercial structures
- Separation of active and future commercial models
- Multi-currency capability and country-by-country activation
- Attribution, correction, reporting, audit, and financial controls
- Unresolved matters that must not be invented

This Founder Decision must be read together with FD-001 and FD-020 through FD-027.

# PART A — REVENUE TERMINOLOGY

## 2. Gross Transaction Value

Gross Transaction Value means:

> The total commercial value of a customer, client, member, participant, sponsor, advertiser, venue, vendor, or partner transaction before approved exclusions, taxes, discounts, credits, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, or adjustments.

Gross Transaction Value may include membership value, Tag value, event value, Marketplace transaction value, Enterprise project value, advertising value, premium-listing value, sponsorship value, administrative fees, franchise or partner-pack fees, training value, technology-service value, or another Founder-approved commercial amount.

Gross Transaction Value is not automatically GCE revenue.

## 3. Collected Amount

Collected Amount means:

> Money successfully received through an approved GCE payment or banking workflow and confirmed through the payment provider, bank, or authorised finance reconciliation.

The following are not collected amounts:

- Failed, expired, reversed, or cancelled payments
- Unpaid invoices
- Proposals, quotations, or forecasts
- Verbal commitments
- Purchase orders without payment
- Pending bank transfers
- Post-dated cheques
- Disputed or unconfirmed collections
- Personal-account collections

## 4. Eligible Revenue

Eligible Revenue means:

> The approved amount on which a platform commission, stakeholder entitlement, subscription commission, revenue share, or other commercial calculation may be based.

General operating formula:

```text
Eligible Revenue
=
Collected Commercial Amount
− GST and statutory taxes
− Refunds
− Reversals
− Chargebacks
− Invalid or fraudulent amounts
− Excluded credits
− Founder-approved non-commissionable components
```

Each vertical may define a more specific eligible-revenue base under an approved Founder Decision.

## 5. Platform Revenue

Platform Revenue means:

> The amount earned by GCE under an approved subscription, fee, commission, sponsorship, advertising, premium-placement, technology, or commercial arrangement.

Platform Revenue is not the same as Gross Transaction Value.

## 6. Settlement-Eligible Amount

Settlement-Eligible Amount means:

> The amount cleared for payout after collection, reconciliation, attribution, fulfilment or milestone validation, tax treatment, holds, dispute checks, reversal checks, approval, and settlement rules are completed.

Payment collection does not automatically create settlement eligibility. Settlement remains governed by FD-020 and FD-021.

# PART B — OPERATING REVENUE RECOGNITION

## 7. Recognition Principle

GCE should operationally recognise revenue only when:

- Payment is successfully collected
- The earning obligation is substantially completed
- Correct attribution is established
- The relevant membership, Tag, event, service, campaign, project milestone, advertisement, sponsorship obligation, or commercial entitlement is activated or fulfilled
- No unresolved fraud, dispute, refund, reversal, chargeback, or compliance hold exists
- The applicable commercial rate is determinable
- Required evidence and approvals are complete

This is an operating and platform-control rule. Statutory accounting remains subject to applicable standards, Chartered Accountant advice, GST, TDS, audit, and country-specific requirements.

## 8. Collected but Unearned Amount

Where payment is received but the earning obligation is incomplete:

- Record it as collected but unearned
- Do not treat it as earned Platform Revenue
- Do not release stakeholder commission
- Do not release settlement
- Resolve it through activation, fulfilment, refund, or exception workflow

## 9. Pipeline Values

Lead value, proposal value, quotation value, expected project value, unsigned contracts, uncollected invoices, forecasts, and unconfirmed sponsorship or advertising bookings may appear in pipeline analytics but are not recognised revenue.

# PART C — GCE CONNECT REVENUE

## 10. GCE Connect Revenue Categories

GCE Connect revenue may include:

- Associate Tier membership subscriptions and renewals
- Future Core Tier upgrade payments and renewals
- Tag 3 and Tag 4 subscriptions
- Paid-event revenue
- Training, workshop, and masterclass revenue
- Approved administrative services
- Approved digital products or tools
- Other Founder-approved Connect services

## 11. Membership Recognition

Membership revenue becomes operationally recognised after:

- Successful payment
- Membership activation
- Correct member and Circle attribution
- No unresolved payment-failure, reversal, or compliance condition

Renewal revenue is recognised after payment and renewal activation.

For a Core upgrade, only the actual collected upgrade amount is recognised, not the full Core price a second time.

## 12. Unactivated Membership Payment

A collected but unactivated membership payment must remain unearned and non-commissionable until activation or refund.

## 13. Grace Period

Grace-period status does not create new revenue or commission without a collected and activated renewal.

## 14. Tag Revenue

Tag 3 and Tag 4 are GCE Connect Tag Subscription Revenue.

Recognition requires:

- Approved Tag
- Successful payment
- Tag activation
- Correct membership attribution
- No unresolved refund, reversal, or hold

A pending Tag request is not revenue.

## 15. Connect BDP Commission

Under FD-025, the Connect BDP earns:

> 20% of eligible GCE Connect subscription revenue

This may include eligible Associate subscription, Associate renewal, Core upgrade, Core renewal, Tag 3, and Tag 4 revenue after collection, activation, attribution, and settlement eligibility.

## 16. Non-Subscription Connect Revenue

Event fees, training fees, administrative fees, advertising, premium listings, sponsorships, technology services, and Lead Assist revenue do not automatically use the Connect BDP subscription-commission rule.

---

# PART D — GCE MARKETPLACE REVENUE

## 17. Active Marketplace Model

The active Marketplace split is:

```text
Eligible Marketplace Revenue
→ 80% Venue Partner entitlement
→ 20% GCE platform commission
```

## 18. Affiliate Is Future Only

No active Marketplace Affiliate model exists under FD-028.

No Affiliate commission, rate, entitlement, attribution, or settlement rule is active. A future Affiliate model requires a separate Founder Decision.

## 19. Marketplace Recognition

Marketplace revenue may be recognised only when:

- Venue identity is verified
- Event, offer, or campaign is valid
- Payment is collected through an approved channel
- The transaction is genuine
- Required fulfilment or redemption is satisfied
- Refunds and reversals are accounted for
- No compliance or dispute hold exists

Self-listing does not remove platform verification or financial controls.

## 20. Minimum Campaign Value

The approved minimum Marketplace campaign commercial value is:

> ₹50,000

It is a minimum approved campaign value, not guaranteed collected revenue and not automatically recognised revenue.

## 21. Marketplace BDP Franchise or Partner Fee

Marketplace BDP Franchise or Partner Fee is an approved revenue category in principle.

Its amount, payment structure, refundability, financing, renewal, territory or capacity rights, commission treatment, recovery treatment, and tax treatment remain Pending Founder Approval.

# PART E — GCE ENTERPRISE REVENUE

## 22. Enterprise Minimum Project Value

The minimum Enterprise project value is:

> ₹1,00,000 excluding GST and statutory taxes

## 23. Enterprise Platform Commission

- Standard rate: 20% of eligible Enterprise event revenue
- Reduced rate: 15%–19% with authorised approval
- Below 15%: special approval required

## 24. Formula

```text
Eligible Enterprise Event Revenue
× Approved Platform Commission Rate
= Eligible Enterprise Platform Commission
```

## 25. Enterprise BDP Commission

The Enterprise BDP earns:

> 25% of the eligible platform commission actually earned by GCE

The Enterprise BDP does not receive 25% of total project value.

## 26. Milestone Recognition

Enterprise revenue should be recognised by approved milestone. The standard 30% / 40% / 30% payment pattern is not inflexible.

Each instalment becomes earned only after collection, milestone approval, valid attribution, determinable platform commission, and clearance of unresolved holds.

## 27. Vendor Opportunity Fee

The Vendor Opportunity Fee concept is approved, but its rate and distribution are unresolved.

It must remain separate from Enterprise platform commission and Enterprise BDP commission and must not be activated until separately approved.

# PART F — ADVERTISING AND PROMOTIONAL VISIBILITY

## 28. Advertising Revenue

Advertising Revenue is an approved category.

Possible products may include:

- Banner, category-page, city-page, event-page, and dashboard advertisements
- Sponsored notifications
- Newsletter or communication sponsorship
- Search-result promotion

Paid advertising must be clearly labelled:

> Sponsored

Advertising must not influence Trust Rank, Core eligibility, taxonomy approval, Circle-seat eligibility, compliance decisions, or organic referral-routing integrity.

## 29. Promotional Visibility Revenue

Premium listing and paid visibility are classified as:

> Promotional Visibility Revenue

Possible products may include Featured Venue, Featured Event, Featured Offer, Featured Business Profile, search priority, homepage placement, category spotlight, city spotlight, and time-limited campaign placement.

Premium visibility purchases exposure only. It does not guarantee leads, referrals, sales, bookings, ranking, approval, Trust Rank, or Core eligibility.

Premium placement cannot bypass verification, taxonomy, seat, relevance, or compliance rules.

---

# PART G — SPONSORSHIP REVENUE

## 30. Sponsorship Revenue

Sponsorship Revenue is an approved independent category.

Possible forms include event title sponsorship, co-sponsorship, session sponsorship, venue sponsorship, category sponsorship, city sponsorship, Circle-event sponsorship, digital-event sponsorship, hospitality, merchandise, technology, and community sponsorship.

## 31. Cash and In-Kind Sponsorship

Sponsorship may be cash, in-kind, or mixed.

Cash sponsorship may be recognised after contract approval, collection, attribution, fulfilment of approved sponsor obligations, and clearance of holds.

In-kind sponsorship must not be shown as cash revenue. It must be recorded separately as:

> In-Kind Sponsorship Value

The record should preserve sponsor, agreed value, goods or services, delivered benefit, evidence, approval, tax treatment, and audit history.

---

# PART H — OTHER COMMERCIAL REVENUE

## 32. Administrative Fee Revenue

Administrative Fee Revenue may include approved transfer, verification, document-processing, or service fees.

The ₹1,000 additional transfer fee under FD-027 is Administrative Fee Revenue, separate from membership revenue and not automatically Connect BDP commissionable.

## 33. Training, Workshop, and Masterclass Revenue

Training, workshops, masterclasses, certification, and educational products are approved revenue categories. Product pricing, refunds, commissions, and tax treatment require specific approval.

## 34. Technology and Digital-Service Revenue

This may include SaaS tools, dashboards, analytics, premium software features, digital workflows, white-label services, or other Founder-approved technology products.

## 35. Ticketing or Booking Service Revenue

Approved booking, convenience, ticketing, reservation, or service fees may be separate revenue streams. Exact commercial treatment remains unresolved.

# PART I — FRANCHISE AND PARTNER-PACK FEES

## 36. Separate Revenue Category

Franchise, unit, and partner-pack fees must be reported separately from membership, event, Marketplace, and Enterprise transaction revenue.

Categories may include:

- Connect BDP Franchise Unit Fee
- Marketplace BDP Franchise or Partner Unit Fee
- Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack Fee
- Other Founder-approved partner fees

## 37. Connect BDP Franchise Unit

Under FD-025:

> ₹50,000 per unit, upfront, one-time, and non-refundable after activation

## 38. Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack

Under FD-026:

- Direct pack: ₹30,000 upfront
- Financed pack: ₹36,000 total
- Initial payment: ₹5,000
- Recoverable balance: ₹31,000
- Maximum monthly recovery: up to ₹5,000
- Recovery only from earned and approved Enterprise BDP commission
- No compulsory monthly cash shortfall
- Balance carries forward

## 39. Marketplace BDP Fee

Marketplace BDP Franchise or Partner Fee remains a revenue category in principle only. Its commercial model is unresolved.

## 40. Accounting Separation

Franchise and partner fee records should distinguish upfront amount, financed amount, recoverable balance, refundable amount, non-refundable earned amount, taxes, receivable, commission recovery, and current liability or receivable status.

A recoverable financed balance is not event revenue.

# PART J — DEPOSITS AND REMOVED ZBP MODEL

## 41. Refundable Deposits

A refundable security or partner deposit is a liability, not revenue, until it is refunded, contractually adjusted, legally forfeited, or converted through another approved event.

## 42. No ZBP Model

No ZBP role, ZBP commission, ZBP security deposit, or ZBP example is active under FD-028.

Old ZBP references in living documents are obsolete.

---

# PART K — LEAD ASSIST

## 43. Lead Assist Separation

Lead Assist remains a separate future commercial service.

FD-028 does not activate a ₹500 fee, escrow, validation fee, success fee, forfeiture, voucher, subscription credit, or Lead Assist commission.

Until separately approved, it remains:

> Pending Lead Assist Commercial Revenue

---

# PART L — TAXES, DISCOUNTS, CREDITS, AND WALLETS

## 44. GST and Statutory Taxes

GST and statutory taxes must be separately recorded and excluded from Platform Revenue and commission bases unless expressly approved or legally required.

## 45. TDS

Gross entitlement is calculated before TDS. TDS is separately recorded at payout, and dashboards should show gross earned amount, TDS deducted, and net payout.

Exact rates remain Pending Tax Review.

## 46. Discounts

Every discount must identify whether it is venue-funded, GCE-funded, shared, sponsor-funded, or another approved type.

The commission base should normally use actual customer-paid eligible commercial value unless a contract defines another approved base.

## 47. Coupons and Credits

Coupons and credits do not automatically count as collected money.

Each must identify its funding source and purpose. A credit with no funding source must not create revenue.

## 48. Wallet Credits

A wallet credit may represent a refund liability, promotional liability, earned balance, pending commission, settlement payable, membership credit, Lead Assist credit, recovery, or manual adjustment.

A wallet entry is not automatically revenue.

---

# PART M — REFUNDS, REVERSALS, AND CHARGEBACKS

## 49. Refund Treatment

A refund reverses the related eligible revenue, Platform Revenue, and stakeholder commission proportionately.

Historical records must be preserved.

## 50. Paid Commission Recovery

If commission was already paid, create a recoverable negative balance and offset it against future earnings where legally and contractually permitted.

## 51. Partial Refund

A partial refund reverses only the proportional revenue and commissions while preserving the original transaction and audit trail.

## 52. Chargeback

A chargeback places the amount on hold or reversal, removes settlement eligibility, reverses pending commission, and creates recovery where already paid.

Chargeback fees must be recorded separately and are not event revenue.

## 53. Failed Payments

Failed, expired, reversed, and cancelled payments are not revenue.

Exact payment-status enums remain Pending Technical Design.

---

# PART N — PAYMENT CHANNELS AND ATTRIBUTION

## 54. Approved Payment Channels

Revenue should normally be recognised only through approved platform-connected payment or banking channels.

Exceptional bank transfers require proof, reconciliation, finance approval, audited entry, and no personal-account collection.

Cash collection is not a standard launch workflow.

## 55. Required Attribution

Every revenue record should be attributable, where applicable, to:

- Vertical and revenue category
- Transaction and counterparty
- Circle, membership, Business Specialization, and Tags
- Venue, event, project, City Unit, or campaign
- Connect BDP Franchise Unit
- Enterprise BDP Franchise Pack
- Marketplace BDP
- Vendor
- Payment, invoice, currency, tax record
- Refund or reversal
- Commission rule
- Settlement record

Missing required attribution places the amount on hold.

## 56. Attribution Correction

Attribution errors must be corrected through a new audit entry. Historical attribution must not be silently overwritten.

# PART P — COMMISSION, MULTI-CURRENCY, AND REPORTING

## 59. Commission States

Commission should conceptually distinguish:

- Estimated
- Pending
- Provisionally calculated
- Earned
- On hold
- Reversed
- Settlement eligible
- Paid
- Recoverable

A dashboard estimate is not earned commission.

## 60. Calculation Order

```text
Gross Transaction Value
→ Excluded taxes
→ Discounts and credits
→ Refunds and reversals
→ Eligible Revenue
→ Platform commission
→ Stakeholder commission
→ Holds and deductions
→ Settlement-Eligible Amount
→ Net payout
```

## 61. Multi-Currency Capability

GCE shall use a:

> Multi-currency-capable financial architecture

The platform must not be permanently restricted to INR.

## 62. Country-by-Country Activation

A currency may be commercially activated only after approval of payment-provider support, banking, settlement, invoicing, taxation, refunds, chargebacks, FX treatment, regulations, payout capability, and Founder approval.

INR may initially be used as the domestic transaction and internal reporting currency.

## 63. Currency Dimensions

The platform should distinguish:

- Transaction currency
- Settlement currency
- Reporting currency
- Stakeholder payout currency

## 64. FX Records

For multi-currency transactions, preserve:

- Original amount and currency
- Exchange rate and source
- Conversion timestamp
- Converted amount
- Settlement currency
- FX fee
- Payment-provider fee
- Refund exchange rate
- Chargeback currency
- Rounding adjustment

Historical transactions must not be recalculated using later exchange rates.

## 65. Commission Currency

Commission should first be calculated in the approved transaction or contractual settlement currency. Reporting-currency conversion must not replace the original-currency record.

## 66. Money Storage and Rounding

Money should be stored in the smallest currency unit, without floating-point calculation. Exact rounding policy remains Pending Technical and Finance Approval.

## 67. Reports

Reports should separately show:

- Gross Transaction Value
- Collected Amount
- Eligible Revenue
- Platform Revenue
- Stakeholder entitlement
- Refunds, reversals, and chargebacks
- Taxes and TDS
- Pending and paid settlement
- Recoverable balances
- Net retained platform amount
- Original and reporting currencies

Dashboards must not use one unqualified “Revenue” figure where it could mislead.

# PART Q — AUDIT, PROHIBITIONS, AND UNRESOLVED ITEMS

## 68. Audit Requirements

Every revenue-impacting action should record actor, timestamp, transaction, original amount and currency, adjusted amount, reason, approval authority, rule version, previous state, new state, evidence, settlement, recovery, refund, and reversal.

No financial record should be hard-deleted.

## 69. Versioned Rules

Revenue and commission calculations must remain linked to the commercial-rule version applicable at the transaction time.

Later rule changes must not silently recalculate historical transactions.

## 70. Prohibited Interpretations

- Gross Transaction Value equals Platform Revenue
- Collected money is automatically earned or settlement eligible
- Proposal value is revenue
- Tax is GCE revenue
- Wallet credit is automatically revenue
- In-kind sponsorship is cash revenue
- Premium listing guarantees results
- Advertising affects Trust Rank or Core eligibility
- Affiliate commission is active
- ZBP exists in the current model
- Marketplace BDP fee details are final
- Lead Assist commercial rules are active
- Vendor Opportunity Fee rate is final

- INR is the only currency the platform may support
- Every technically supported currency is commercially active
- Refunds can occur without revenue and commission reversal
- Historical attribution may be overwritten
- Personal-account collection is allowed
- Recoverable franchise balances are event revenue
- Transfer fees are automatically Connect BDP commissionable

## 74. Decision Statement

GCE shall operate through a revenue architecture that clearly separates Gross Transaction Value, Collected Amount, Eligible Revenue, Platform Revenue, and Settlement-Eligible Amount.

Revenue shall be operationally recognised only after collection, valid attribution, completion of the applicable earning condition, and clearance of unresolved refund, reversal, dispute, fraud, tax, and compliance holds.

GCE may generate revenue through memberships, Tags, Marketplace platform commission, Enterprise platform commission, events, training, advertising, premium listings, sponsorships, administrative fees, franchise and partner-pack fees, ticketing, technology services, and other Founder-approved products.

The active Marketplace model is 80% Venue Partner entitlement and 20% GCE platform commission. No Affiliate commission is active. Marketplace BDP Franchise or Partner Fee remains unresolved beyond its recognition as a potential revenue category.

ZBP is not part of the current model.

The platform shall be technically multi-currency capable. Additional currencies may be activated country by country after Founder, finance, legal, tax, banking, regulatory, settlement, and payment-provider approval. Original transaction currency and historical FX data must be preserved.

This decision remains active until explicitly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
