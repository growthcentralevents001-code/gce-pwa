# FD-030 — GCE Connect Circle Architecture and Governance

**Decision ID:** FD-030
**Title:** GCE Connect Circle Architecture and Governance
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** Circle Structure, Governance, Meetings, Verification, Seats, Taxonomy, Conduct, and Learning Constitution
**Authority Level:** Founder Decision
**Platform:** Growth Central Events (GCE)

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision defines the internal architecture and governance of every GCE Connect Circle.

It covers:

- Circle ownership and capacity
- Circle activation thresholds
- GC Power Sectors
- Business Specializations
- Protected Tag Scope
- Business Tags
- Minimum business-verification conditions
- Governing Body structure and authority
- Physical-first phygital meetings
- Attendance, visitors, and substitutes
- Referrals and dual-confirmed closed business
- Conduct, complaints, discipline, and corrective action
- Seat reservation, renewal, freeze, transfer, vacancy, waitlist, merger, and closure
- Expert speakers and optional paid business-skill workshops
- Digital governance, access control, reporting, and audit

This decision must be read with FD-001, FD-022, FD-023, FD-024, FD-025, FD-027, FD-028, and FD-029.

FD-024 remains the primary authority for the Circle lifecycle. FD-030 governs the Circle’s internal structure and operation.

# PART A — CIRCLE IDENTITY AND CAPACITY

## 2. Definition

A GCE Connect Circle is:

> A platform-governed local business networking unit consisting of up to 40 active physical members, organised through approved Business Specializations, Business Tags, GC Power Sectors, referral workflows, meetings, and Circle governance.

A Circle is not an independently owned association or member-owned database.

## 3. Platform Ownership

GCE owns and controls:

- Circle identity and records
- Membership data
- Business taxonomy
- Business Specializations
- Protected Tag Scope
- Business Tags
- Referral and closed-business records
- Governing Body permissions
- Circle activation, suspension, merger, and closure
- Financial, commercial, and audit records

The Connect BDP manages and supports the Circle but does not own it.

## 4. Circle Capacity

The maximum capacity is:

> **40 active physical members per Circle**

Rules:

- One active membership normally equals one primary physical Circle seat.
- A Circle may not exceed 40 active seats.
- A member may not hold two primary seats in the same Circle.
- A member normally belongs to one primary Circle.
- Tags and digital access do not create extra physical seats.
- Pending, suspended, expired, frozen, and waitlisted users do not count as active seats except where an approved seat-protection rule applies.

## 5. Circle Stages

1. Proposed Circle
2. Formation Circle
3. Provisionally Active Circle
4. Fully Constituted Circle
5. Performance Review
6. Suspended Circle
7. Merged Circle
8. Closed Circle

## 6. Activation Thresholds

- Fewer than 20 approved and paid members: Formation Circle
- 20–39 approved and paid members: Provisionally Active Circle
- 40 approved and paid members: Fully Constituted Circle

Activation does not remove verification, payment, seat-eligibility, or platform-approval requirements.

# PART B — GC POWER SECTORS

## 7. Four Fixed GC Power Sectors

Every Circle is organised under:

1. **Real Estate, Infrastructure & Construction Sector**
2. **Industrial, Manufacturing & Logistics Sector**
3. **Professional, Financial & Business Services Sector**
4. **Consumer, Hospitality, Health & Lifestyle Sector**

These are platform taxonomy structures, not separate Circles.

## 8. Flexible Sector Distribution

The four Power Sectors remain fixed, but the 40 seats are not rigidly divided 10/10/10/10.

Rules:

- Every Specialization belongs to one primary Power Sector.
- Seat distribution may vary according to verified local demand.
- Platform taxonomy should prevent one sector from dominating the Circle.
- Final seat approval remains subject to Circle balance and conflict review.

# PART C — SPECIALIZATION, PROTECTED TAG SCOPE, AND TAGS

## 9. Business Specialization

Business Specialization means:

> The member’s primary approved business category under which the physical Circle seat is allocated and protected.

Rules:

- One member has one primary Business Specialization.
- One protected Specialization normally occupies one physical seat.
- Two active members should not hold the same protected Specialization in the same Circle.
- The Specialization must reflect the member’s verified principal business activity.
- Platform taxonomy gives final approval.
- Broad or misleading categories may not be used to block competitors.
- Historical Specialization records must be preserved.

## 10. Protected Tag Scope

Protected Tag Scope means:

> The approved commercial boundary of the member’s protected Circle seat.

It helps determine protected activities, allowed overlap, Tag eligibility, and referral relevance.

It does not automatically cover every service the member offers.

## 11. Business Tags

Approved rules:

- Maximum four active Tags
- First two Tags included in base membership
- Tag 3 costs 25% of the active base subscription
- Tag 4 costs 25% of the active base subscription
- The earlier 50% fourth-Tag rule is obsolete
- Tags do not create additional physical seats

## 12. Tag Controls

- Tags require platform-approved taxonomy.
- Tags must reflect verified capability.
- Tags may not disguise a second unrelated primary business.
- Tags may not override another member’s Protected Tag Scope.
- Tag misuse may cause rejection, suspension, or removal.
- Tag 3 and Tag 4 require approval and payment.
- Membership expiry, suspension, or termination affects Tag status.

## 13. Overlap Review

1. Applicant selects a Business Specialization.
2. Platform checks existing protected seats.
3. Connect BDP verifies the business.
4. Protected Tag Scope is assessed.
5. Platform determines no conflict, compatible alternate Specialization, limited overlap, hold, or rejection.
6. Decision and reason are recorded.

Final authority remains with the platform.

# PART D — BUSINESS VERIFICATION MINIMUM CONDITIONS

## 14. Verification Principle

No Circle seat is activated until identity, authority, business existence, operating capability, Specialization eligibility, and compliance checks are completed through platform-recorded evidence.

Verification must use a risk-based multi-evidence approach. GST registration alone is not the only test of legitimacy.

## 15. Identity and Authority

Minimum checks include:

- Government identity proof
- PAN or applicable tax identity
- Current address and contact details
- Proof that the applicant is an owner, partner, director, authorised representative, or responsible decision-maker
- Authorisation letter where applicable

## 16. Business Existence

At least one reliable record should be provided where applicable, such as:

- GST registration
- Udyam registration
- Company or LLP registration
- Shop and Establishment registration
- Trade or professional licence
- Partnership deed
- Business bank-account proof
- Recent invoice
- Other legally acceptable business proof

Absence of one specific document does not automatically invalidate a genuine business where other reliable evidence exists.

## 17. Operating-Business Verification

The Connect BDP should verify genuine operations through one or more of:

- Physical visit
- Live video verification
- Official website or verified business profile
- Recent invoices or transactions
- Business signage and location evidence
- Products, services, machinery, inventory, staff, or operating setup
- Customer or supplier reference where required

## 18. Specialization Eligibility

The verifier must confirm:

- The selected Specialization is the principal activity.
- The applicant has actual capability and experience.
- The category is not selected merely to block another member.
- The business does not materially conflict with an existing protected seat.
- Requested Tags reflect genuine capabilities.
- Required licences are valid.
- The stated activity is legally permitted.

## 19. Reputation and Compliance

Minimum checks include:

- No known fraudulent or misleading representation
- No serious unresolved complaint creating material platform risk
- Valid required professional licences
- No prohibited or illegal activity
- Acceptance of GCE conduct, confidentiality, referral, payment, and data rules
- Disclosure of relevant conflicts

## 20. Verification Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | Required checks completed; applicant may proceed |
| Conditionally Verified | Minor non-critical item pending; temporary approval with deadline |
| On Hold | Material evidence, compliance, or conflict issue requires review |
| Rejected | Legality, ownership, authenticity, capability, or eligibility not established |

## 21. Verification Record

The platform should preserve:

- Documents reviewed
- Verification method
- Visit or video result
- Verified Specialization
- Approved Tags
- Risk observations
- Verifier
- Date
- Evidence
- Conditions
- Re-verification date
- Decision and reason

Re-verification may be required after ownership, activity, location, Specialization, licence, or serious compliance change.

# PART E — GOVERNING BODY

## 22. Purpose

The Governing Body supports Circle operations, meeting quality, participation, engagement, referral discipline, and escalation.

It does not own the Circle.

## 23. Core Roles

1. Circle President
2. Circle Vice President
3. Secretary
4. Circle Finance Coordinator
5. Sergeant at Arms
6. Membership and Growth Coordinator
7. Referral and Performance Coordinator

Compatible roles may be combined temporarily in a Formation Circle with platform approval.

## 24. Governing Body Term

> **Six months**

Reappointment is subject to performance and platform approval.

## 25. Eligibility

A member should normally have:

- Active membership
- Current KYC and business verification
- Satisfactory attendance
- Satisfactory conduct
- No serious disciplinary hold
- No unresolved fraud or material financial dispute
- Required orientation
- Consent to serve

Exact minimum tenure remains unresolved.

## 26. Appointment Model

- Eligible members may nominate or vote.
- Connect BDP verifies eligibility.
- Platform confirms appointment.
- Platform may reject or remove an appointment for compliance, conflict, misconduct, or governance risk.

## 27. Role Responsibilities

### Circle President

- Chairs meetings
- Coordinates governance
- Maintains discipline
- Escalates issues
- Supports engagement

Cannot independently suspend, terminate, fine, refund, or settle a member.

### Circle Vice President

- Supports the President
- Monitors attendance and participation
- Coordinates Power Sector activity
- Supports corrective plans

### Secretary

- Maintains records, agenda, minutes, resolutions, and action items
- Updates authorised platform records

### Circle Finance Coordinator

- Explains platform-recorded financial reports
- Reviews approved expenses
- Identifies reconciliation issues
- Supports event-budget transparency

Must not collect fees personally, hold Circle funds, approve their own reimbursement, alter commission, release settlement, or operate an unauthorised Circle bank account.

### Sergeant at Arms

- Manages punctuality, attendance discipline, agenda timing, visitor protocol, speaking order, and meeting behaviour

Cannot independently impose punishment.

### Membership and Growth Coordinator

- Supports visitor onboarding, vacancies, retention, orientation, and balanced growth

Final verification remains with the Connect BDP and platform.

### Referral and Performance Coordinator

- Promotes platform-recorded referrals
- Supports dual-confirmation discipline
- Reviews participation and referral quality
- Escalates suspicious records

Cannot alter referral values.

## 28. Conflict of Interest

A Governing Body member must disclose business, family, financial, referral, vendor, event, or complaint-related conflicts and must not participate in the affected decision.

## 29. Governing Body Limitations

The Governing Body cannot independently:

- Change fees or commission
- Change taxonomy
- Approve final Specializations or Tags
- Activate or terminate membership
- Forfeit fees
- Change wallet balances
- Reverse commission
- Release settlement
- Open unauthorised bank accounts
- Override platform decisions

# PART F — GCE PHYGITAL CIRCLE MEETING FRAMEWORK

## 30. Core Principle

Physical meetings every 15 days remain the core GCE Connect relationship format.

Digital systems support preparation, attendance, referral recording, action tracking, training, and exceptional remote participation but must not replace or dilute physical relationship-building.

The approved name is:

> **GCE Phygital Circle Meeting Framework**

## 31. Meeting Frequency

- Standard meetings occur every 15 days.
- Two standard meetings per month should occur where the calendar permits.
- Physical participation remains primary.
- Additional digital committee, orientation, Power Sector, and training sessions may occur separately.

## 32. Pre-Meeting Digital Workflow

The platform may collect:

- Attendance confirmation
- Leave request
- Business introduction update
- Referral requirement
- Opportunity offered
- Presentation material
- Visitor details
- Agenda suggestion
- Previous action status

## 33. Digital Meeting Companion

An authorised operator may record:

- Attendance
- Visitors
- Referrals
- Action items
- Meeting notes
- Power Sector commitments
- Follow-up deadlines

Members should remain physically engaged rather than spending the meeting on devices.

## 34. Post-Meeting Digital Workflow

The platform may send:

- Meeting summary
- Referrals received
- Follow-up tasks
- Action deadlines
- Workshop announcements
- Next meeting date
- Attendance status
- Feedback form

## 35. Remote Participation

Remote participation may be approved for medical reason, essential business travel, emergency, temporary absence from city, or another platform-approved reason.

Safeguards:

- At least 75% of standard Circle meetings should be attended physically by the member personally.
- Remote attendance is separately labelled.
- Remote and substitute attendance do not fully replace physical participation.
- A Circle may not convert all standard meetings to online meetings for convenience.
- Digital-only sessions may be used for training, orientation, committee work, or urgent matters.

## 36. Standard Meeting Agenda

1. Welcome and attendance
2. Visitor and new-member introduction
3. Member business introductions
4. Referral updates
5. Dual-confirmed closed-business updates
6. Power Sector collaboration
7. Featured member presentation
8. Training or platform update
9. Governing Body announcements
10. Upcoming events
11. Issues and action items
12. Closing summary

# PART G — ATTENDANCE, SUBSTITUTES, AND VISITORS

## 37. Attendance Standard

- Expected attendance: at least 75%
- Two consecutive unexplained absences: warning
- Three consecutive unexplained absences: formal review
- Below 60% over a rolling three-month period: Corrective Action Plan
- Continued failure after corrective action: possible suspension or seat review

Attendance alone does not automatically terminate membership.

## 38. Valid Absence Reasons

- Health emergency
- Family emergency
- Essential business travel
- Religious or public obligation
- Platform-approved reason

## 39. Substitute Attendance

A verified authorised representative may attend on limited occasions.

- Substitute attendance is separately recorded.
- It does not automatically count as full personal attendance.
- Substitute cannot vote unless separately authorised.
- Substitute must follow confidentiality and conduct rules.
- Repeated substitution cannot replace personal participation.

Exact substitute limits remain unresolved.

## 40. Visitors

- Invitation should be recorded.
- Identity and business details should be collected.
- Specialization conflicts should be checked.
- Visitor cannot represent themselves as a member.
- Visitor cannot access confidential data.
- Follow-up occurs through authorised onboarding.
- Visitor attendance does not reserve a seat.

---

# PART H — REFERRALS AND CLOSED BUSINESS

## 41. Official Referral Record

A referral becomes part of official Circle performance only when recorded through the platform.

Records should include lead giver, lead receiver, date, type, relevant Specialization or Tag, status, follow-up, outcome, and closed-business confirmation where applicable.

## 42. Dual-Confirmed Closed Business

1. Lead giver enters the business amount.
2. Lead receiver confirms it.
3. Only then does it become part of the official digital ledger.

No unilateral final amount is permitted. Disputed amounts remain pending. Corrections require audit history.

## 43. Referral Quality

The platform should track accepted, rejected, relevant, invalid, conversion, follow-up time, dual-confirmed closed business, disputes, and repeated low-quality referrals.

Referral volume alone is not the only performance measure.

# PART I — EXPERT SPEAKERS AND PAID WORKSHOPS

## 44. Program Name

> **GCE Circle Business Growth Workshops**

These optional paid workshops may improve sales, negotiation, marketing, finance, customer retention, leadership, legal awareness, tax awareness, AI adoption, presentation, referral conversion, branding, team management, expansion, and sector-specific capability.

## 45. Workshop Formats

1. Circle-exclusive workshop
2. Multi-Circle workshop
3. GC Power Sector masterclass
4. Platform-wide digital masterclass
5. Premium small-group business clinic

## 46. Eligible Speakers

Speakers may include industry experts, entrepreneurs, chartered accountants, lawyers, marketers, sales trainers, technology experts, HR specialists, sector experts, and verified GCE members with relevant expertise.

## 47. Speaker Verification

GCE should verify:

- Identity
- Credentials
- Relevant experience
- Past speaking or training work
- References
- Conflict of interest
- Proposed content
- Fee
- No misleading income guarantee
- No undisclosed aggressive selling
- Data and confidentiality compliance

## 48. Workshop Approval Workflow

1. Members identify skill need.
2. Governing Body recommends topic.
3. Connect BDP reviews relevance.
4. Platform identifies or approves speaker.
5. Speaker submits agenda and commercial proposal.
6. Platform approves price, capacity, refund rule, and delivery mode.
7. Workshop is listed.
8. Members purchase through approved platform channel.
9. Attendance is recorded.
10. Feedback and rating are collected.
11. Speaker payment is released after approved delivery.

## 49. Pricing Models

- Per-member ticket
- Fixed Circle booking fee
- Platform-sponsored or subsidised program

No universal workshop price is fixed under FD-030.

## 50. Commercial Controls

- Payments use approved platform channels.
- Speaker fee must be contractually recorded.
- Platform fee, taxes, refunds, venue cost, and expenses are separately shown.
- Connect BDP does not automatically receive 20% of workshop revenue.
- Governing Body does not automatically receive a share.
- No workshop commission may be inferred without separate Founder approval.
- Speaker payment depends on approved delivery and settlement conditions.

## 51. Optional Participation

Paid workshops are normally optional.

Mandatory sessions should be limited to approved orientation, compliance, or platform training that is included in membership or separately disclosed.

## 52. Recommended Learning Calendar

- One short skill segment inside one physical meeting each month
- One optional paid expert workshop every one or two months
- One quarterly Power Sector masterclass
- One quarterly platform-wide business growth summit
- Recordings or summaries where rights permit

## 53. Member-Led Workshops

Verified members may serve as speakers if expertise is relevant, conflict is disclosed, content is educational, pricing follows the same process, and quality standards remain equal.

# PART J — CONDUCT, COMPLAINTS, AND DISCIPLINE

## 54. Conduct Standards

Members must not:

- Harass or threaten
- Misrepresent capability
- Make false referral claims
- Manipulate closed-business figures
- Misuse customer data
- Bypass payment controls
- Collect unauthorised fees
- Discriminate unlawfully
- Repeatedly disrupt meetings
- Damage the GCE brand
- Create unofficial competing records
- Misrepresent authority

## 55. Progressive Discipline

1. Informal guidance
2. Written warning
3. Corrective Action Plan
4. Temporary restriction
5. Suspension
6. Membership or seat termination

Serious fraud, violence, harassment, data theft, financial misconduct, or legal risk may bypass earlier stages.

## 56. Corrective Action Plan

A plan should include the issue, evidence, required correction, responsible person, review period, support, consequence, reviewer, and outcome.

No Circle officer may unilaterally terminate a member.

## 57. Complaint Process

1. Complaint submitted
2. Acknowledgement
3. Conflict check
4. Evidence collection
5. Temporary protection where needed
6. Response opportunity
7. Authorised review
8. Decision and reason
9. Appeal where permitted
10. Audit record

# PART K — AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

## 58. Connect BDP Authority

The Connect BDP verifies businesses, explains membership, forms and supports Circles, coordinates onboarding, maintains Circle health, supports governance, reviews engagement, recommends corrective measures, and escalates disputes.

The Connect BDP cannot own the Circle, override Founder Decisions, change fees or commissions, guarantee business, alter financial records, approve their own commission exception, or permanently control member data.

## 59. Platform Authority

GCE retains final authority over Circle creation, identity, activation, membership status, taxonomy, Specializations, Tags, Protected Tag Scope, seat conflicts, governance permissions, suspension, termination, merger, closure, data access, finance, commission, settlement, appeals, compliance, and rule interpretation.

# PART L — SEATS, RENEWAL, FREEZE, TRANSFER, AND VACANCY

## 60. Seat Reservation

> **Seven days**

Reservation begins after preliminary approval. Payment and onboarding must be completed within the period. Reservation is not activation. Extension requires authorised approval.

## 61. Renewal and Grace

- Renewal notice: 30 days
- Grace period: 30 days
- Seat remains active until expiry.
- Grace-period participation may be restricted.
- After grace expiry, the seat may become vacant.
- Rejoining does not automatically restore an already reallocated seat.

## 62. Membership Freeze

- Freeze may be approved for up to 90 days.
- Seat protection is recommended for up to 30 days.
- After 30 days, the platform may review continued protection.
- Freeze does not erase obligations or create another seat.

## 63. Circle Transfer

- First transfer within 12 months: free
- Later transfer: ₹1,000 plus tax

Requires valid reason, compatible vacancy, Specialization review, Protected Tag Scope review, effective date, preserved history, and platform approval.

## 64. Specialization Change

Requires updated verification, taxonomy review, conflict check, Protected Tag Scope review, Tag review, Connect BDP recommendation, platform approval, effective date, and audit trail.

## 65. Vacant Seats and Waitlist

Vacancies must be published through authorised platform process.

Waitlist position does not guarantee admission. Verification, Circle balance, strategic need, and final platform approval still apply.

# PART M — MERGER, CLOSURE, AND PERFORMANCE

## 66. Circle Merger

A merger may be considered for unsustainably low membership, repeated meeting failure, governance breakdown, insufficient local demand, complementary nearby vacancies, or platform strategy.

Membership, referral, closed-business, financial, disciplinary, and attribution history must be preserved.

## 67. Circle Closure

Closure may result from inactivity, insufficient membership, governance failure, legal or compliance risk, market restructuring, platform strategy, or merger.

Closure does not automatically cancel active memberships.

## 68. Circle Performance

Indicators may include active members, seat occupancy, retention, renewal, attendance, referral participation, referral quality, dual-confirmed closed business, visitor conversion, governance compliance, complaint levels, sector balance, engagement, events, and data completeness.

No single metric automatically determines closure.

# PART N — FINANCIAL, DIGITAL, AND VOTING CONTROLS

## 69. Financial Controls

- Payments use approved platform channels.
- No personal collection account.
- No unauthorised cash pool.
- No Circle bank account without Founder and legal approval.
- Approved expenses must be recorded.
- No self-approval of reimbursement.
- Commission and settlement follow FD-020, FD-021, FD-028, and FD-029.

## 70. Digital Governance Records

The platform should maintain the roster, seat map, Specialization map, Tags, governance appointments, attendance, referrals, closed-business confirmations, complaints, warnings, corrective plans, transfers, vacancies, freeze, suspension, Circle status, and audit history.

## 71. Access Control

Access follows FD-023.

Members, Governing Body, Connect BDP, Finance, and administrators receive only role-appropriate access.

## 72. Voting

Voting may be used for advisory matters and Governing Body nominations.

Voting cannot independently determine membership approval, taxonomy, Specialization conflict, fees, commission, refunds, suspension, termination, data access, Circle closure, or platform policy.

Exact quorum and voting percentages remain unresolved.

GCE provides networking infrastructure, referral tools, visibility, participation, governance, and opportunity access.

## 76. Decision Statement

GCE Connect Circles shall remain physical-first, platform-governed business networking units with a maximum of 40 active physical members.

A Circle may become Provisionally Active at 20 approved and paid members and Fully Constituted at 40.

Every Circle shall operate through four fixed GC Power Sectors, one protected primary Business Specialization per member, platform-controlled Protected Tag Scope, and a maximum of four Business Tags.

No Circle seat may be activated without platform-recorded business verification covering identity, authority, business existence, operating capability, Specialization eligibility, and compliance.

Standard Circle meetings shall occur every 15 days under the GCE Phygital Circle Meeting Framework. Digital tools may improve preparation, attendance, referral records, actions, training, and exceptional remote participation, but they must not replace or dilute physical relationship-building.

GCE may conduct optional paid GCE Circle Business Growth Workshops using verified expert speakers and platform-controlled commercial workflows. Workshop pricing, speaker fees, refunds, platform revenue, and stakeholder commissions require separate approval and must not be inferred from membership or BDP commission rules.

The Governing Body supports operations but does not own the Circle or control membership, taxonomy, finance, commission, settlement, suspension, termination, merger, or closure.

GCE retains final authority over Circle identity, data, seats, taxonomy, governance permissions, compliance, finance, and rule interpretation.

This decision remains active until explicitly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
