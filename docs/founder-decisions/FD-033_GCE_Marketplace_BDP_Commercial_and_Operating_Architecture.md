# PART A — ROLE DEFINITION

## 2. Marketplace BDP Definition

A Marketplace BDP is an approved GCE commercial and operating partner responsible for:

- Identifying eligible Venue Partners
- Conducting initial venue assessment
- Coordinating venue verification
- Onboarding approved Venue Partners
- Acting as the primary Relationship Manager for onboarded venues
- Supporting Marketplace Event and Marketplace Offer Event readiness
- Maintaining venue information
- Supporting platform adoption
- Supporting offer and event listing readiness
- Coordinating issue resolution
- Supporting legitimate Marketplace revenue generation
- Preserving platform rules, customer rights, and venue accountability

A Marketplace BDP is not:

- The owner of GCE Marketplace
- The owner of a Venue Partner
- The legal owner of customer data
- A permanent owner of a city
- A payment gateway
- A settlement authority
- A tax authority
- A platform administrator
- A Venue Partner’s employee
- An independent right to alter prices, refunds, commission, or platform policy
- A Marketplace Affiliate

---

## 3. Canonical Role Name

The canonical business-facing role is:

> **GCE Marketplace Business Development Partner**

Approved short form:

> **Marketplace BDP**

Legacy abbreviation:

> **MBDP**

The legacy abbreviation may remain in historical records or technical migration mappings, but all new business-facing documentation should use Marketplace BDP.

# PART B — APPOINTMENT AND ELIGIBILITY

## 4. Appointment Authority

Only GCE may appoint a Marketplace BDP.

Appointment requires:

- Platform approval
- Completion of applicable KYC
- Identity verification
- Business or professional-background review
- Acceptance of Marketplace BDP agreement
- Acceptance of confidentiality and data-protection obligations
- Acceptance of commission and recovery rules
- Completion of required onboarding or training
- Payment or approved finance activation under FD-029
- No disqualifying conflict, fraud, or serious compliance issue

Payment of the package alone does not create appointment.

---

## 5. Eligibility Principles

An applicant should normally demonstrate:

- Local business-development capability
- Ability to identify and engage Venue Partners
- Basic understanding of hospitality, venues, events, offers, or local commerce
- Ability to use GCE digital systems
- Ability to maintain accurate records
- Professional communication
- Compliance discipline
- No known pattern of fraud or misrepresentation
- Ability to provide reasonable support to onboarded venues

GCE may reject or hold an application where verification is incomplete or risk is unacceptable.

---

## 6. Appointment Outcomes

Application outcomes may include:

- Approved
- Conditionally Approved
- On Hold
- Rejected
- Withdrawn

Conditional approval may require specified documents, training, payment, verification, or compliance steps before activation.

# PART C — MARKETPLACE BDP FRANCHISE UNIT

## 7. Unit Definition

A Marketplace BDP Franchise Unit is the approved commercial and operating capacity through which a Marketplace BDP may onboard and manage Venue Partners.

One unit includes capacity for:

> **Up to 20 active Venue Partners**

The unit is a capacity entitlement, not a guarantee that 20 venues will be allocated or activated.

---

## 8. Active Venue Partner

For unit-capacity purposes, an Active Venue Partner is a venue that:

- Has completed approved onboarding
- Has current verification
- Has an active platform relationship
- Has not been terminated
- Has not been permanently deactivated
- Is assigned to the Marketplace BDP
- Is capable of participating in applicable Marketplace activity

A venue may remain active even when it has no current event or offer, provided its platform relationship and verification remain active.

---

## 9. Capacity Treatment

The following rules apply:

- One unit supports up to 20 active Venue Partners.
- Pending applications do not automatically consume permanent active capacity.
- Rejected, withdrawn, or terminated venues do not consume active capacity.
- Temporarily inactive venues may continue to consume capacity during an approved inactivity or remediation period.
- Capacity is released only after approved closure, transfer, or termination.
- A Marketplace BDP may not exceed approved capacity through duplicate or informal accounts.

---

## 10. Maximum Units Per Person

A Marketplace BDP may hold:

> **A maximum of two active Marketplace BDP Franchise Units**

Therefore, the standard maximum capacity per Marketplace BDP is:

> **40 active Venue Partners**

A second unit is not automatic.

It requires:

- First unit substantially utilised
- Good compliance standing
- Acceptable venue-support quality
- Acceptable data accuracy
- No serious unresolved disputes
- Platform approval
- Applicable package activation
- Operational capacity to manage additional venues

Any capacity above two units requires a separate Founder-approved exception or future Founder Decision.

---

# PART D — CITY AND OPERATING-AREA MODEL

## 11. No Permanent City Ownership

A Marketplace BDP does not receive permanent ownership of a city, zone, district, or market.

Marketplace BDP operations are:

> **Venue-attribution based, not permanently territory-owned**

GCE may appoint multiple Marketplace BDPs in the same city according to:

- Venue demand
- Market size
- Venue density
- Service quality
- Capacity
- Performance
- Platform strategy
- Compliance

---

## 12. Operating Area

A Marketplace BDP may be assigned or approved for:

- A city
- A city cluster
- A district
- A locality
- A market segment
- A venue category
- A specific venue portfolio

The operating area is for coordination and accountability.

It does not create permanent monopoly rights.

---

## 13. Performance-Protected Venue Attribution

A Marketplace BDP’s validly onboarded venue remains attributed to that Marketplace BDP while:

- The Marketplace BDP remains active
- The venue relationship remains active
- Service obligations are reasonably fulfilled
- No transfer, termination, or reassignment event occurs
- No fraud or attribution manipulation is established

GCE must not reassign an active venue without a recorded reason and due process.

---

# PART E — VENUE PARTNER ONBOARDING AUTHORITY

## 14. Sole Onboarding Channel

New Marketplace Venue Partners shall ordinarily be onboarded through an approved Marketplace BDP.

The Marketplace BDP that validly onboards the Venue Partner becomes:

> **The primary Relationship Manager for that venue**

No separate parallel Relationship Manager commission layer is created.

GCE retains final approval authority.

---

## 15. Venue Onboarding Workflow

The standard workflow is:

1. Venue identified
2. Venue interest recorded
3. Venue consent obtained
4. Venue profile created
5. Ownership or operating authority verified
6. KYC and due diligence completed
7. Venue category and service capability recorded
8. Location and capacity details recorded
9. Commercial terms accepted
10. Venue Partner agreement completed
11. Platform approval recorded
12. Venue assigned to Marketplace BDP
13. Venue activated
14. Onboarding and support initiated

---

## 16. Venue Verification

Verification may include:

- Legal or business identity
- Ownership or authorised operating control
- Address
- Contact details
- Bank or settlement information where required
- Tax information where legally required
- Venue photographs
- Capacity
- Facilities
- Licences
- Safety or statutory documents where applicable
- Category eligibility
- Platform policy acceptance

No single document is absolute proof in every case.

GCE may require additional evidence.

---

## 17. Venue Onboarding Outcomes

Outcomes may include:

- Verified and Approved
- Conditionally Approved
- On Hold
- Rejected
- Withdrawn

A rejected venue does not create commission or permanent attribution.

---

# PART F — MARKETPLACE BDP RELATIONSHIP MANAGER DUTIES

## 18. Primary Relationship Manager

The Marketplace BDP that onboards a venue is its primary Relationship Manager for normal Marketplace operations.

Duties include:

- Helping the venue understand Marketplace features
- Supporting event and offer readiness
- Supporting accurate venue information
- Coordinating issue escalation
- Encouraging timely responses
- Supporting platform compliance
- Monitoring venue activity
- Supporting renewals or reactivation where applicable
- Helping the venue understand settlement and platform workflow without changing finance rules
- Coordinating with GCE support, finance, and compliance teams

---

## 19. Relationship Manager Boundaries

The Marketplace BDP may not:

- Approve refunds
- Release settlements
- Alter commission
- Change tax treatment
- Change customer rank
- Approve its own exception
- Alter platform revenue
- Collect off-platform platform fees unless specifically authorised
- Hold customer money personally
- Promise guaranteed sales
- Promise guaranteed events
- Promise guaranteed customer footfall
- Suppress venue complaints
- Conceal poor venue performance
- Transfer a venue informally
- Sell venue or customer data

---

# PART H — COMMISSION AND REVENUE ENTITLEMENT

## 24. Marketplace Revenue Split

For Eligible Marketplace Event Revenue:

| Stakeholder | Share |
|---|---:|
| Venue Partner | 80% |
| Marketplace BDP | 10% |
| GCE net retained share | 10% |

The Marketplace BDP’s 10% entitlement is subject to:

- Valid venue attribution
- Eligible Marketplace transaction
- Revenue recognition
- No disqualifying refund, reversal, chargeback, fraud, or hold
- Commission approval
- Settlement eligibility

---

## 25. Commission Base

The Marketplace BDP earns:

> **10% of Eligible Marketplace Event Revenue**

It does not automatically earn 10% of:

- Gross enquiry value
- Unpaid booking value
- Cancelled transaction value
- Refunded amount
- Tax
- Security deposit
- Refundable amount
- Enterprise project value
- Connect subscription revenue
- Lead Assist revenue
- Sponsorship
- Advertising
- Training
- Workshop revenue
- Any non-approved revenue stream

---

## 26. Attribution Requirement

Marketplace BDP commission requires valid venue attribution.

The record should preserve:

- Marketplace BDP
- Marketplace BDP unit
- Venue Partner
- Onboarding date
- Activation date
- Attribution start
- Attribution status
- Reassignment history
- Revenue event
- Commission rule version
- Settlement state

---

## 27. No Automatic Commission

A Marketplace BDP does not earn commission merely for:

- Finding a venue
- Creating a draft profile
- Submitting KYC
- Scheduling a meeting
- Listing an unapproved venue
- Creating an enquiry
- Assisting a venue without eligible revenue
- Referring a venue that was already actively attributed
- Claiming ownership without evidence

Commission arises only under the approved revenue and entitlement rules.

# PART I — PERFORMANCE AND MAINTENANCE

## 28. Operating Objective

The Marketplace BDP’s primary objective is:

> To onboard, activate, support, and maintain a healthy portfolio of verified Venue Partners that can participate in legitimate GCE Marketplace activity.

---

## 29. Performance Indicators

Performance may be assessed using:

- Verified venues onboarded
- Active Venue Partners
- Venue profile completeness
- Venue response time
- Event and offer readiness
- Venue retention
- Customer complaints
- Venue complaints
- Data accuracy
- Compliance
- Settlement cooperation
- Platform adoption
- Valid Marketplace revenue
- Venue inactivity
- Dispute rate
- Misconduct or fraud indicators

Revenue alone must not be the only performance measure.

---

## 30. Initial Performance Review

The first unit should normally be reviewed during the first ten months.

The review should consider:

- Venue onboarding quality
- Active portfolio growth
- Support quality
- Compliance
- Data accuracy
- Marketplace activity
- Venue retention
- Ability to responsibly manage capacity

FD-033 does not create a guaranteed minimum income.

---

## 31. Recommended Portfolio Milestone

A Marketplace BDP should normally aim to build:

> **Up to 20 active Venue Partners within ten months per unit**

This is an operating performance objective, not a guarantee that 20 suitable venues exist or will be approved.

The platform may adjust operational pacing based on:

- City size
- Venue density
- Venue category
- Market readiness
- Seasonal conditions
- Quality and compliance considerations

No unsuitable venue should be approved merely to meet a target.

---

## 32. Maintenance Duty

After building the portfolio, the Marketplace BDP must:

- Maintain venue relationships
- Keep records current
- Support inactive venues
- Assist with compliance remediation
- Prevent duplicate venue accounts
- Support customer and venue issue resolution
- Coordinate platform updates
- Avoid misleading commercial claims

---

# PART J — ADDITIONAL UNIT ELIGIBILITY

## 33. Second Unit

A second Marketplace BDP unit may be approved where:

- The first unit is near practical capacity
- Venue support quality is acceptable
- No serious unresolved compliance issue exists
- Venue data is current
- Commission recoveries are being handled under approved rules
- The Marketplace BDP has capacity to manage more venues
- Platform demand supports expansion
- GCE grants approval

Payment alone does not guarantee a second unit.

---

## 34. No Automatic Portfolio Splitting

A Marketplace BDP may not:

- Split one venue into multiple accounts
- Create shell venues
- Use duplicate legal entities to inflate capacity
- Assign inactive or fake venues to consume capacity
- Shift venues between units to manipulate targets
- Create false transactions to qualify for expansion

---

# PART K — VENUE INACTIVITY

## 35. Inactive Venue States

A Venue Partner may become:

- Temporarily Inactive
- Under Remediation
- Suspended
- Voluntarily Paused
- Terminated
- Transferred

Exact technical enums may be designed in Phase 2, but the business meaning must remain distinct.

---

## 36. Temporary Inactivity

A venue may be temporarily inactive due to:

- Seasonal closure
- Renovation
- Licence renewal
- Management transition
- Temporary compliance issue
- Repeated non-response
- Venue-requested pause
- Platform review

Temporary inactivity does not automatically end Marketplace BDP attribution.

---

## 37. Inactivity Review

The Marketplace BDP should:

- Contact the venue
- Record the reason
- Update the status
- Support remediation
- Escalate compliance issues
- Avoid keeping permanently dead venues artificially active

GCE may review whether the inactive venue continues to consume unit capacity.

---

# PART L — VENUE REASSIGNMENT AND TRANSFER

## 38. Reassignment Grounds

A venue may be reassigned where:

- Marketplace BDP resigns
- Marketplace BDP is suspended or terminated
- Marketplace BDP is persistently non-responsive
- Service quality materially fails
- Venue requests reassignment with valid reason
- Conflict of interest exists
- Attribution was fraudulent
- Venue was incorrectly assigned
- Portfolio restructuring is required
- Both parties agree and GCE approves

---

## 39. Reassignment Due Process

Before reassignment, GCE should normally:

1. Record the reason
2. Notify affected parties
3. Collect relevant evidence
4. Allow a response where appropriate
5. Decide attribution
6. Record effective date
7. Preserve prior attribution history
8. Assign the new Marketplace BDP or platform Relationship Manager
9. Update data access
10. Preserve financial audit records

---

## 40. Commission Cut-Off

Commission treatment before and after venue reassignment must follow:

- Revenue event date
- Earning event
- Valid attribution date
- Reassignment effective date
- Refund or reversal
- Commission rule version
- Settlement status

A reassignment does not automatically remove already earned and approved commission.

A Marketplace BDP does not automatically retain commission on future revenue after attribution ends.

---

## 41. No Sale of Venue Attribution

Venue attribution:

- Cannot be sold privately
- Cannot be transferred for personal consideration
- Cannot be pledged
- Cannot be inherited automatically
- Cannot be assigned without GCE approval

---

# PART M — SUSPENSION, TERMINATION, AND EXIT

## 42. Suspension Grounds

A Marketplace BDP may be suspended for:

- Serious verification failure
- Fraud suspicion
- Data misuse
- Customer or venue harassment
- Misrepresentation
- Unauthorised fee collection
- Off-platform diversion
- Manipulated transactions
- Fake venue creation
- Repeated non-response
- Serious compliance breach
- Confidentiality breach
- Conflict-of-interest concealment
- Failure to support assigned venues
- Failure to cooperate with investigation

Suspension is not final termination unless separately decided.

---

## 43. Suspension Effects

During suspension:

- New venue onboarding may stop
- New venue assignment may stop
- Data access may be restricted
- Commission may be held where permitted
- Existing venues may receive temporary platform support
- Investigation may continue
- Already earned commission remains subject to FD-029 and due process

---

## 44. Termination Grounds

Termination may occur for:

- Proven fraud
- Serious repeated misconduct
- Material data breach
- Unauthorised collection or diversion of money
- Repeated platform-policy violations
- Falsified KYC
- Fake venues
- Manipulated revenue
- Selling data
- Abandonment of duties
- Serious reputational harm
- Breach of Marketplace BDP agreement
- Failure to remedy a serious suspension issue

---

## 45. Voluntary Exit

A Marketplace BDP may request voluntary exit.

Exit requires:

- Written or platform-recorded notice
- Handover
- Return or revocation of platform access
- Venue communication
- Settlement review
- Recovery-balance treatment
- Data deletion or retention under approved policy
- Closure of active disputes

Exact notice period remains Pending Legal and Operational Approval.

---

## 46. Handover

Handover must include:

- Venue portfolio
- Venue contact history
- Open events and offers
- Active complaints
- Pending compliance items
- Pending settlements
- Attribution records
- Documentation
- Open disputes
- Data-access revocation
- Successor assignment

No venue should lose platform support solely because a Marketplace BDP exits.

---

# PART N — DATA, PRIVACY, AND ACCESS

## 47. Data Access Principle

Marketplace BDP access must be limited to information reasonably required for assigned venues and approved operations.

Access must be:

- Role-based
- Purpose-limited
- Logged
- Revocable
- Restricted after reassignment or exit

---

## 48. Prohibited Data Use

A Marketplace BDP must not:

- Sell venue data
- Sell customer data
- Export data for unrelated business
- Use customer contact data for unauthorised marketing
- Share credentials
- Download data without authority
- Retain data after access ends unless legally required
- Use GCE data to build a competing database
- Contact reassigned venues for disruption or diversion

---

## 49. Data After Exit

After suspension, termination, or exit:

- Access should be revoked or reduced
- Required records remain with GCE
- Personal copies must be deleted where required
- Audit records remain preserved
- Legal-retention requirements continue
- Historical attribution remains visible to authorised users

Exact retention periods remain Pending Legal and Privacy Review.

---

# PART O — MISCONDUCT AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST

## 50. Prohibited Conduct

A Marketplace BDP must not:

- Promise guaranteed business
- Promise guaranteed income
- Promise guaranteed venue bookings
- Charge unauthorised fees
- Collect platform money personally
- Falsify venue data
- Create fake bookings
- Manipulate customer reviews
- Hide complaints
- Divert Venue Partners off-platform
- Divert customers off-platform
- Resell leads
- Claim another BDP’s venue
- Harass venues
- Discriminate unlawfully
- Offer hidden kickbacks
- Share confidential commission data improperly
- Misuse GCE trademarks
- Present itself as the legal owner of GCE

---

## 51. Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure

A Marketplace BDP must disclose where it:

- Owns a venue
- Is employed by a venue
- Has a close financial relationship with a venue
- Receives outside compensation
- Is involved in a competing platform
- Has a personal interest in a transaction
- Is selecting among related venues

Disclosure does not automatically prohibit participation, but GCE may impose controls.

## 69. Decision Statement

GCE Marketplace shall operate through approved Marketplace BDP Franchise Units.

A Marketplace BDP is responsible for identifying, onboarding, activating, supporting, and maintaining verified Venue Partners and acts as the primary Relationship Manager for validly onboarded venues.

One Marketplace BDP unit may support up to 20 active Venue Partners.

A Marketplace BDP may hold a maximum of two active units, subject to performance, compliance, platform demand, and separate approval.

Marketplace BDP operations are venue-attribution based and do not create permanent ownership of a city or territory.

FD-029 remains authoritative for the approved package, finance, commission, recovery, revenue, and settlement rules.

For Eligible Marketplace Event Revenue, the approved distribution is:

- Venue Partner: 80%
- Marketplace BDP: 10%
- GCE net retained share: 10%

A Marketplace BDP has no automatic entitlement to Connect, Enterprise, Lead Assist, sponsorship, advertising, workshop, or other revenue.

Marketplace Affiliate remains inactive.

Venue attribution, commission, recovery, reassignment, suspension, termination, and handover must remain platform-controlled, rule-based, and auditable.

All unresolved legal, tax, privacy, finance, operational, and technical matters remain unresolved and must not be invented during Phase 2.

This decision remains active until expressly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
