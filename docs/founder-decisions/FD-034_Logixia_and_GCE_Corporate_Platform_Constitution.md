# FD-034 — Logixia and GCE Corporate Platform Constitution

**Decision ID:** FD-034
**Title:** Logixia and GCE Corporate Platform Constitution
**Status:** Founder Approved
**Decision Type:** Corporate Identity, Platform Ownership, Brand Architecture, Contracting Authority, Intellectual Property, Data Governance, Stakeholder Relationship, and Future Restructuring Constitution
**Authority Level:** Founder Decision
**Legal Entity:** Logixia Solutions Private Limited
**Platform and Master Brand:** Growth Central Events (GCE)
**Applies To:** Logixia Solutions Private Limited, Growth Central Events, GCE Connect, GCE Marketplace, GCE Enterprise, all future GCE sub-verticals, BDPs, Venue Partners, members, customers, Enterprise clients, vendors, employees, consultants, contractors, platform teams, finance teams, legal teams, data-protection teams, investors, and technical implementation teams

---

## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision establishes the constitutional relationship between:

> **Logixia Solutions Private Limited**

and

> **Growth Central Events (GCE)**

It defines:

- The legal company
- The platform and master brand
- Ownership and operating relationship
- Brand and vertical hierarchy
- Contracting authority
- Payment and invoicing principles
- Intellectual-property ownership
- Data-governance position
- Stakeholder contractual relationships
- Enterprise platform-versus-executor distinction
- Authority over BDP appointments
- Authority over Venue Partner and Enterprise relationships
- Future subsidiary, investment, licensing, and restructuring principles
- Phase 2 legal-entity and technical implementation boundaries

This decision does not replace statutory corporate documents, legal agreements, tax advice, privacy notices, or professional legal opinions.

It establishes the Founder-approved business constitution that those documents must implement.

This decision must be read together with FD-001 and FD-020 through FD-033.

# PART A — CONSTITUTIONAL IDENTITY

## 2. Legal Company

The Founder-approved legal company is:

> **Logixia Solutions Private Limited**

Logixia Solutions Private Limited is intended to be the legal company that owns, operates, manages, commercialises, and develops the Growth Central Events platform, subject to:

- Valid incorporation
- Applicable statutory filings
- Corporate approvals
- Tax registration
- Banking and payment-provider approval
- Legal documentation
- Data-protection compliance
- Intellectual-property registration or assignment

---

## 3. Platform and Master Brand

The Founder-approved platform and master brand is:

> **Growth Central Events**

Approved short form:

> **GCE**

GCE is:

- A platform
- A master business brand
- A technology-enabled business ecosystem
- A product and operating division under Logixia Solutions Private Limited
- The parent platform for approved GCE verticals and sub-verticals

GCE is not currently intended to be treated as a separate legal company unless a later Founder Decision and corporate action establish a separate legal entity.

# PART C — BRAND AND VERTICAL ARCHITECTURE

## 9. Master Brand Structure

GCE is the master platform brand.

1. GCE Connect
2. GCE Marketplace
3. GCE Enterprise

These verticals must remain distinct in business logic, commercial rules, stakeholder entitlements, user experience, and reporting.

---

## 10. Parent-Child Brand Rule

A sub-brand or program must remain under its approved parent vertical.

It must not be presented as a separate top-level GCE vertical unless a later Founder Decision approves that change.

Examples:

- A Connect program remains within GCE Connect.
- A Marketplace Offer Event remains within GCE Marketplace.
- An Enterprise service remains within GCE Enterprise.

---

## 11. Brand Ownership and Use

Only Logixia Solutions Private Limited or an expressly authorised party may:

- Register GCE trademarks
- Register domains
- Create official sub-brands
- License the GCE name
- Approve co-branding
- Authorise franchise branding
- Authorise BDP branding
- Approve investor or partner use
- Approve white-label use

BDPs, Venue Partners, members, vendors, and contractors receive only limited, revocable permission to use approved GCE branding.

They do not acquire ownership of the brand.

---

## 12. Prohibited Brand Representation

No stakeholder may represent that they:

- Own GCE
- Own a GCE vertical
- Are a legal subsidiary unless formally established
- Are authorised to bind Logixia without written authority
- Are an employee unless actually employed
- Are an exclusive city owner unless expressly approved
- Are a legal partner in the company merely because they are a BDP, member, vendor, or Venue Partner

## 62. Decision Statement

Logixia Solutions Private Limited is the Founder-approved legal company intended to own, operate, manage, commercialise, and develop the Growth Central Events platform.

Growth Central Events, or GCE, is the Founder-approved platform and master business brand operated under Logixia Solutions Private Limited.

GCE is not currently a separate legal company.

The approved platform structure is:

- GCE Connect
- GCE Marketplace
- GCE Enterprise

Subject to professional validation and formal corporate documentation, Logixia should ordinarily be the contracting entity, payment-receiving entity, invoice-issuing entity, platform operator, and owner or controller of GCE intellectual property.

BDPs, members, Venue Partners, vendors, consultants, and Governing Body members do not acquire company ownership, platform ownership, legal partnership, or authority to bind Logixia merely through their platform role.

GCE Enterprise ordinarily acts as a technology, procurement, matching, governance, commercial-workflow, and financial-control platform, while approved vendors ordinarily perform physical execution.

All statutory corporate documents, legal agreements, tax treatment, privacy classifications, payment structures, and regulatory matters remain subject to qualified professional review and formal approval.

All unresolved matters remain unresolved and must not be invented during Phase 2.

This decision remains active until expressly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision or valid corporate action.
