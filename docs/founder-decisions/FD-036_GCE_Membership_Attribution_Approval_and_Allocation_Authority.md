## 1. Purpose

This Founder Decision establishes the authoritative operating model for GCE Connect membership approval, activation, attribution, Circle allocation, waitlisting, Relationship Manager assignment, Circle transfer, and geographic routing.

It resolves the outstanding questions concerning:

- Who may become a GCE Connect member
- Whether membership activation and Circle allocation are the same event
- Who approves membership
- What conditions must be completed before membership becomes Active
- Who allocates a member to a Circle
- How Connect BDP attribution is created
- Whether a member may exist temporarily without Connect BDP attribution
- How unattributed membership revenue is treated
- Whether Connect BDP attribution can be backdated
- Whether Relationship Manager assignment creates commission
- What happens when the desired category seat is unavailable
- How category and Tag selection fit into onboarding
- How Circle transfers work
- How geographic matching should operate
- How exceptions and manual overrides are controlled
- How all of the above remain auditable

This decision does not redefine:

- Membership prices already approved
- Tag prices already approved
- Circle capacity
- Circle lifecycle or constitutional status
- Connect BDP commission percentage
- Revenue recognition
- Settlement rules
- Governing Body tenure
- AI Lead Assist commercial activation

Those remain governed by their existing Founder Decisions.

# PART A — MEMBERSHIP IDENTITY AND ELIGIBILITY

## 2. Membership Is a Scoped GCE Connect Relationship

GCE Connect Membership is an approved commercial and participation relationship between GCE and an eligible User.

Membership does not replace the User’s permanent base identity.

Under FD-035:

> **User remains the permanent base identity.**

Membership is represented as a separate GCE Connect entitlement and role assignment.

---

## 3. Individual Seat Principle

A Circle seat is held by an approved natural person representing an eligible business or professional practice.

The platform should preserve:

- Member identity
- Represented business
- Business specialisation
- Approved category
- Approved Tags
- Circle
- Membership plan
- Membership status
- Seat status

A company may be represented by an authorised individual, but the Circle seat itself is not an unrestricted transferable corporate asset.

Transfer to another representative requires an approved membership and seat-change process.

---

## 4. Membership Does Not Guarantee a Specific Circle Seat

Payment alone does not guarantee:

- A particular Circle
- A particular category seat
- A particular business specialisation
- A particular geographic area
- A particular Connect BDP
- Immediate allocation

Membership approval and seat allocation are related but distinct processes.

---

# PART B — MEMBERSHIP ONBOARDING ORDER

## 5. Approved Golden Path

The approved standard onboarding sequence is:

1. User registration
2. Basic profile completion
3. Business or professional details
4. Initial city / geographic preference
5. Initial business category / specialisation selection
6. Initial Tag selection
7. Seat-availability check
8. Membership plan selection
9. Terms acceptance
10. Required KYC / business verification
11. Payment
12. Membership activation
13. Circle allocation
14. Final seat confirmation
15. Connect BDP attribution confirmation
16. Workspace activation
17. Member onboarding completion

The platform may combine some screens for user convenience, but the business sequence and audit states must remain distinguishable.

---

## 6. Category and Tags Before Purchase

The member should select:

- Intended business category or specialisation
- Initial Tags
- Preferred city / Circle geography

before final membership purchase confirmation.

Purpose:

- Prevent sale into an obviously unavailable category
- Improve Circle allocation accuracy
- Reduce refunds and manual remediation
- Allow category and Tag validation before seat commitment

Final approval may still change the proposed category or Tags with member consent where required.

---

## 7. Seat Check Before Payment

Before payment, the system should attempt to determine whether:

- A suitable seat is immediately available
- A suitable seat exists in another Circle
- A waitlist is required
- The case requires manual review

The platform must not falsely represent a seat as guaranteed until allocation is formally confirmed.

---

# PART C — MEMBERSHIP APPROVAL AUTHORITY

## 8. Membership Approval Is Platform-Controlled

Final membership activation authority belongs to GCE through an approved platform workflow.

The following actors may participate:

- Automated eligibility checks
- Connect BDP
- Platform Operations
- Compliance
- Support
- Governing Body where a Circle-specific governance input is required

But no single local actor may independently override platform rules.

---

## 9. Connect BDP Role in Membership Approval

The Connect BDP may:

- Assist prospective members
- Verify business information
- Confirm commercial legitimacy
- Recommend category and Tag fit
- Help identify a suitable Circle
- Support documentation completion
- Flag conflicts or duplication
- Recommend approval or hold

The Connect BDP may not:

- Self-approve their own membership
- Override payment status
- Override KYC failure
- Override platform capacity
- Bypass category exclusivity
- Change subscription pricing
- Create commission by manual attribution without evidence

---

## 10. Governing Body Role

The Governing Body may provide Circle-specific input regarding:

- Local category conflicts
- Circle conduct concerns
- Local operational suitability
- Governance concerns
- Requests for taxonomy review

The Governing Body does not have unilateral authority to:

- Approve platform payment
- Override KYC
- Change membership price
- Change platform taxonomy
- Override seat-capacity rules
- Create or remove Connect BDP commission
- Bind Logixia Solutions Private Limited

---

## 11. Platform Operations Final Authority

Platform Operations retains final authority to:

- Activate membership
- Place membership on hold
- Reject membership where required
- Approve manual exception
- Confirm Circle allocation
- Reassign an unresolved application
- Escalate compliance matters
- Correct attribution errors
- Resolve conflicts between operational actors

All manual decisions must be auditable.

---

# PART D — MEMBERSHIP ACTIVATION

## 12. Membership Activation and Circle Allocation Are Separate

Approved principle:

> **Membership activation and Circle allocation are separate business states.**

A member may have an Active membership even if final Circle allocation is still pending.

This allows the platform to support:

- Organic signups
- Temporary waitlist
- Category-seat search
- Reassignment
- Cross-Circle allocation review
- Platform-led onboarding

---

## 13. Conditions for Active Membership

Membership may become Active after:

- User identity is valid
- Required profile details are complete
- Applicable KYC / business verification is complete
- Membership terms are accepted
- Payment is confirmed
- No blocking compliance issue exists
- Membership record is created
- Platform activation is recorded

Circle allocation is not required for the membership record itself to become Active.

However, Circle-specific rights may remain restricted until seat allocation is completed.

---

## 14. Rights Before Circle Allocation

An Active member who is not yet allocated to a Circle may receive only approved pre-allocation benefits.

The member must not automatically receive:

- Voting rights in a Circle
- Governing Body eligibility
- Circle-specific attendance credit
- Circle-specific category exclusivity
- Circle-specific internal referral routing
- Circle-specific governance authority

until Circle allocation is complete.

---

## 15. Allocation Target

The platform should normally aim to resolve a standard Circle allocation within:

> **Up to seven business days**

from completion of membership activation and all required allocation inputs.

This is an operating target, not a guaranteed contractual SLA.

Cases involving:

- Category conflict
- City shortage
- Compliance hold
- Member-requested delay
- New Circle formation
- Taxonomy review

may require additional time.

---

# PART E — CIRCLE ALLOCATION AUTHORITY

## 16. Allocation Model

Circle allocation is a platform-controlled process supported by the Connect BDP and system recommendations.

Approved operating model:

> **System proposes → Connect BDP assists → Platform confirms.**

---

## 17. System Role

The system may recommend allocation based on:

- City
- Geographic proximity
- Circle availability
- Category vacancy
- Business specialisation
- Approved Tags
- Capacity
- Member preference
- Conflict rules
- Circle stage

The system recommendation is assistive and does not automatically override Founder-approved rules.

---

## 18. Connect BDP Role in Allocation

The Connect BDP may:

- Review recommended Circle
- Confirm business fit
- Confirm seat availability
- Support category and Tag validation
- Explain Circle structure to the member
- Recommend another Circle where justified

The Connect BDP does not have unrestricted power to create a seat beyond the approved 40-member capacity.

---

## 19. Platform Confirmation

Final Circle allocation becomes effective only when the platform records:

- Member
- Circle
- Seat
- Category or specialisation
- Tags
- Effective date
- Connect BDP attribution where applicable
- Approval authority
- Rule version

# PART F — CONNECT BDP ATTRIBUTION

## 20. Attribution Is Separate From Membership Identity

Connect BDP attribution is a commercial and operating relationship linking an eligible membership to the responsible Connect BDP.

It is not the same as:

- User identity
- Circle membership
- Membership payment
- Circle seat
- Relationship Manager assignment

---

## 21. Valid Attribution

Connect BDP attribution should arise only where there is an approved basis, such as:

- The Connect BDP sourced the member
- The Connect BDP materially onboarded the member
- The member joined through the Connect BDP’s approved Circle-building process
- The platform formally assigned attribution under approved rules

Attribution must be recorded.

---

## 22. Organic / Unattributed Memberships Are Allowed

A User may become an Active GCE Connect member without an immediate Connect BDP attribution.

This may occur through:

- Organic platform signup
- Direct GCE marketing
- Existing GCE relationship
- Referral without eligible Connect BDP attribution
- Temporary allocation delay
- City or Circle transition

The absence of Connect BDP attribution must not prevent legitimate membership activation where all other requirements are satisfied.

---

## 23. No Automatic Connect BDP Commission Without Attribution

Where no valid Connect BDP attribution exists at the relevant earning event:

> **No Connect BDP commission entitlement arises for that transaction.**

The amount that would otherwise have formed part of a Connect BDP entitlement is not described as “unpaid BDP commission.”

It remains with GCE under the applicable revenue architecture unless and until a valid approved attribution rule creates a future entitlement.

---

## 24. No Retroactive Commission by Default

Later assignment of a Connect BDP does not automatically create retroactive commission on earlier membership revenue.

Future eligible revenue may become attributable from the approved effective date.

Retroactive correction is allowed only where:

- The original attribution was a documented platform error; or
- An authorised dispute resolution determines that attribution should have existed earlier.

Any correction must be auditable.

---

## 25. Attribution Effective Date

Every Connect BDP attribution should preserve:

- Member
- Connect BDP
- Relevant unit
- Relevant Circle where applicable
- Attribution reason
- Effective date
- End date
- Source
- Approver
- Evidence
- Rule version

---

# PART G — CONNECT BDP COMMISSION BOUNDARY

## 26. Existing Commission Rules Preserved

FD-036 does not change the Connect BDP commission rate or approved commission base.

Those remain governed by FD-025 and FD-029.

FD-036 only clarifies when attribution exists for commission eligibility.

---

## 27. No Duplicate Attribution

A membership transaction must not produce duplicate Connect BDP entitlement merely because:

- More than one Connect BDP interacted with the member
- A member moved Circles
- A Relationship Manager assisted
- A Governing Body member assisted
- A referral occurred
- A member held multiple roles

Only the approved attribution record controls BDP entitlement.

# PART H — RELATIONSHIP MANAGER ASSIGNMENT

A Relationship Manager may be assigned to support:

- Member onboarding
- Circle issues
- Service coordination
- Escalation
- Retention
- Complex support
- Cross-Circle transition

RM assignment is operational and does not automatically create commission.

## 29. RM Assignment Authority

Connect BDP may recommend RM involvement but does not automatically control RM assignment.

---

## 30. Platform Relationship Manager Escalation

Existing dispute-escalation principles remain active.

# PART I — CATEGORY FULL / WAITLIST FLOW

## 31. Category Seat Full

Where the member’s desired category or specialisation is unavailable in the preferred Circle, the member should not be forced into an incorrect category.

The platform should present approved alternatives.

---

## 32. Allocation Alternatives

The standard order is:

1. Another suitable Circle in the same city
2. Another suitable nearby Circle within the approved geographic hierarchy
3. Waitlist for preferred Circle / category
4. New Circle formation pathway where commercially and operationally justified
5. Manual Platform review

The member should be informed of the available options.

---

## 33. Waitlist

A member may be placed on a waitlist where:

- Desired category seat is unavailable
- Member prefers to wait
- Suitable alternate Circle is unavailable
- New Circle formation is expected
- Platform review is pending

The waitlist must preserve:

- User
- Category
- Tags
- Preferred geography
- Date entered
- Priority basis
- Status
- Offers of alternate allocation
- Exit reason

Exact waitlist prioritisation rules remain Pending Founder or Product Design if not already governed elsewhere.

---

## 34. No Forced Core-Tier Path

Category unavailability must not automatically force a User into a future Core Tier product.

Core Tier remains subject to its separate Founder-approved launch conditions.

---

# PART J — GEOGRAPHIC ROUTING

## 35. Geographic Hierarchy

For membership allocation and Circle matching, the recommended approved hierarchy is:

> **Locality / practical proximity → City → District → State**

The platform should prioritise practical member participation over rigid administrative boundary formalism.

---

## 36. City as Primary Connect Operating Geography

City remains the primary GCE Connect operating geography.

District and State are fallback or reporting layers.

Locality, zone, or proximity may be used operationally within a city.

---

## 37. No Automatic Permanent Territorial Ownership

Geographic routing does not itself grant a Connect BDP permanent ownership of:

- City
- District
- State
- Locality
- Pin-code cluster

Connect BDP operating rights remain governed by FD-025 and approved unit rules.

---

## 38. Geographic Exceptions

The platform may permit allocation across nearby boundaries where:

- Travel is practical
- Member agrees
- Category seat is available
- Circle participation remains viable
- No other rule is breached

---

# PART K — CIRCLE TRANSFER

## 39. Member May Request Transfer

A Member may request transfer from one Circle to another.

Transfer is not automatic.

Reasons may include:

- Residence or business relocation
- Better geographic fit
- Category restructuring
- Circle closure or merger
- Operational conflict
- Member request
- Platform-directed reallocation

---

## 40. Transfer Conditions

Before transfer, the platform should verify:

- Destination capacity
- Destination category availability
- Tag compatibility
- Membership status
- Open disputes
- Governance obligations
- Pending financial issues where relevant
- Attribution implications

---

## 41. Old Seat Release

When transfer becomes effective:

- Old Circle seat is released
- Historical membership remains preserved
- Historical attendance and referral records remain in the old Circle context
- New Circle rights begin from the transfer effective date

No historical record should be silently rewritten.

---

## 42. Connect BDP Attribution on Transfer

Circle transfer does not automatically transfer Connect BDP commission attribution.

The platform must determine whether:

- Existing Connect BDP attribution continues
- New Connect BDP attribution begins
- No Connect BDP attribution applies

based on the approved commercial attribution rule and effective dates.

No duplicate entitlement is allowed.

- Payment initiated

- Membership activation pending

## 47. Activation Recovery

- Activation should retry idempotently where safe
- User should see a clear Pending Activation state
- Support should be able to inspect the case
- Manual remediation must be auditable

Refund rules remain subject to the approved refund policy and financial authority.

## 60. Decision Statement

GCE Connect membership shall operate as a separate entitlement and participation relationship attached to the User’s permanent base identity.

Initial business category, specialisation, Tags, and preferred geography should be selected before final membership purchase so that seat availability can be assessed before payment.

Membership activation and Circle allocation are separate states.

A membership may become Active after required profile, verification, terms, payment, compliance, and platform-activation conditions are completed even if final Circle allocation remains pending.

Circle-specific governance and seat rights begin only after formal allocation.

The standard allocation model is:

> **System proposes → Connect BDP assists → Platform confirms.**

The platform should normally aim to resolve standard allocation within up to seven business days, without treating that target as a guaranteed contractual SLA.

Organic and temporarily unattributed memberships are permitted.

Where no valid Connect BDP attribution exists at the relevant earning event, no Connect BDP commission entitlement arises.

Later Connect BDP assignment does not automatically create retroactive commission.

Relationship Manager assignment is operational and does not automatically create commission.

Where a preferred category seat is unavailable, the platform should first seek another suitable Circle in the same city, then a practical nearby Circle, then waitlist or new-Circle/manual-review options.

Category unavailability does not automatically force a member into Core Tier.

The primary geographic allocation hierarchy is locality or practical proximity, then City, District, and State.

Circle transfers are permitted through controlled approval, capacity, category, attribution, and audit checks.

Business categories, specialisations, and rule-critical Tags shall use controlled GCE taxonomy.

Payment, membership activation, Circle allocation, Connect BDP attribution, RM assignment, waitlist, and transfer must remain distinct and auditable business states.

All unresolved legal, tax, refund, KYC, privacy, SLA, taxonomy-data-model, and technical implementation details remain pending the appropriate authority.

This decision remains active until expressly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
