# PART A — AUTHORITY AND INTERPRETATION

## 2. Founder Business Direction vs Professional Validation

This Founder Decision distinguishes two layers:

### Layer 1 — Founder Approved Business Rule

The Founder determines:

- The intended commercial model
- The preferred operating model
- The product direction
- The stakeholder relationship
- The platform default

### Layer 2 — Professional Validation

Legal, Tax, Privacy, Finance, Banking, and Payment specialists determine:

- How the approved business rule must be implemented lawfully
- Which registrations, disclosures, taxes, controls, contract clauses, or provider configurations are required
- Whether a legal prohibition or material regulatory conflict exists

Professional validation must not silently rewrite approved Founder business rules.

If a genuine prohibition or material conflict is found:

> **The issue must be escalated for Founder review.**

# PART B — MARKETPLACE MERCHANT OF RECORD

## 3. Intended Merchant of Record

For GCE Marketplace event tickets:

> **Logixia Solutions Private Limited shall be the intended Merchant of Record.**

The approved business direction is that Logixia/GCE:

- Collects customer ticket payment through the approved platform payment architecture
- Records the transaction
- Applies the approved revenue and commission rules
- Settles the applicable Venue Partner and Marketplace BDP entitlements
- Maintains the relevant financial audit trail

---

## 4. MoR Direction Is a Business Decision

The Merchant-of-Record business direction is not reopened merely because the following remain pending implementation validation:

- GST treatment
- Invoice structure
- Payment-gateway configuration
- Refund accounting
- TDS or withholding
- Settlement compliance
- Payment-aggregator applicability
- Banking structure

These matters affect implementation, not the Founder’s intended commercial direction.

---

## 5. Professional Validation Gate

Before production go-live of Marketplace ticket money movement, the MoR structure must be validated for:

- Applicable tax treatment
- Invoice issuer and supplier presentation
- GST handling
- Refund and credit-note treatment
- TDS/withholding
- Payment-provider configuration
- Settlement mechanics
- Banking and payment compliance

If validation shows that the intended model is legally prohibited or materially non-compliant, the exact conflict must be brought back for Founder review.

# PART C — BDP LEGAL PACKAGING

## 6. Working Legal Model

The approved working legal architecture for GCE BDP relationships is:

> **Commercial Licence / Independent Business Partner model**

This applies as the drafting direction for:

- Connect BDP
- Marketplace BDP
- Enterprise BDP

unless a later Founder Decision creates a different structure.

---

## 7. “Franchise Unit” Terminology

The term:

> **Franchise Unit**

describes the approved commercial unit/package model.

It does not automatically mean that the legal relationship is a formal franchise relationship.

A legal franchise relationship exists only if the final executed agreement expressly establishes one.

---

## 8. No Automatic Employment or Agency

BDP status does not automatically create:

- Employment
- Partnership
- Agency
- Joint venture
- Fiduciary relationship
- Authority to bind Logixia

Any such legal effect must arise from the final approved agreement and applicable law.

# PART F — EVENT CANCELLATION POLICY

## 14. Default Cancellation Cutoff

The default GCE Marketplace event customer cancellation cutoff is:

> **48 hours before event start**

---

## 15. Event-Specific Variation

An approved event-specific policy may differ from the 48-hour default where:

- The customer is clearly informed before purchase
- The variation is approved
- The variation is operationally reasonable
- The variation is permitted by applicable law and policy

The platform must preserve the applicable policy version for each booking.

---

## 16. Refund Policy Still Separate

FD-039 approves the cancellation cutoff direction.

It does not by itself define:

- Exact refund percentage
- Refund processing time
- Convenience-fee treatment
- Tax reversal treatment
- Chargeback handling
- No-show treatment

Those remain subject to the applicable refund, Finance, Tax, Legal, and Product policy.

# PART H — KYC AND AADHAAR POSTURE

## 21. Data-Minimisation Principle

GCE should minimise identity-document collection.

Approved direction:

> **Aadhaar is not mandatory by default.**

---

## 22. Preferred Verification Approach

The platform should prefer lower-risk and fit-for-purpose verification documents based on stakeholder type, such as:

- PAN
- Driving Licence
- Passport
- Business registration records
- GST records where applicable
- Company documents
- Bank verification
- Other appropriate evidence

---

## 23. Aadhaar Use

Aadhaar may be used only where:

- Legally permitted
- Operationally justified
- Privacy implications are understood
- Collection is proportionate
- Appropriate consent and handling requirements are satisfied

No workflow should require Aadhaar merely because it is technically convenient.

## 42. Decision Statement

Logixia Solutions Private Limited shall be the intended Merchant of Record for GCE Marketplace event tickets.

This Merchant-of-Record business direction remains Founder approved while GST, invoicing, payment-provider, refund, TDS, settlement, and payment-compliance implementation details undergo professional validation.

The approved working legal model for Connect BDP, Marketplace BDP, and Enterprise BDP relationships is a Commercial Licence / Independent Business Partner model. The term “Franchise Unit” describes a commercial unit/package and does not automatically establish a legal franchise relationship.

GCE shall use AI as the primary first-draft legal and compliance drafting assistant and shall maintain an Applicable Law & Compliance Register to map actual applicable Indian laws, rules, policies, and sector-specific requirements. AI drafts must undergo final legal/compliance validation before production publication or binding contractual reliance.

The default GCE Marketplace event customer cancellation cutoff shall be 48 hours before event start, while approved event-specific variations may apply if clearly disclosed before purchase and legally permitted.

BDP commercial-pack payments shall be online by default. Rare bank-based offline payments may be recorded through an authorised, reconciled, and fully audited Admin workflow. Cash is not a normal activation method.

GCE shall minimise Aadhaar collection and shall not make Aadhaar mandatory by default unless a specific legally and operationally justified workflow requires it.

Phase 2 shall include the approved commercial spine across Associate Membership, GCE Connect Circles, Connect BDP, Marketplace Events and Venue Partners, Marketplace BDP, BDP commercial packs, GCE Enterprise, Enterprise BDP, and unpaid Lead Assist Stage 1 foundations.

The first pilot city remains intentionally undecided and shall not block Phase 2 Technical Architecture. Founder selection is required before pilot deployment planning is finalised.

Professional compliance review may block production go-live or contract reliance, but must not silently change approved Founder commercial rules. Any genuine legal prohibition or material compliance conflict must be escalated for Founder review.

This decision remains active until expressly amended or superseded by a later Founder Decision.
