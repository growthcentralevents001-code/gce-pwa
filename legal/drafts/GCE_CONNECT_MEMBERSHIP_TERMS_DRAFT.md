## 1. Nature of membership

Associate Membership is a **platform membership** in GCE Connect. It is not a shareholding in Logixia, not a partnership, and not a guarantee of business.

**Core direct purchase remains inactive.** These terms do not offer Core as a self-serve SKU.

## 2. Pricing

Launch pricing is as published in canonical commercial constants at the time of purchase (see `docs/core/36_Commercial_Constants.md` / FD-027). This draft does not restate every rupee figure so that stale copies do not drift. GST extra as applicable — **CA**.

## 3. Tags, category, specialisation

Tags and category/specialisation follow current Founder rules (including Tag commercial rules in constants). Do **not** apply legacy “Tag 4 +50%” unless that is the current constant — verify against FD-027/constants at execution.

## 4. Circle allocation

Payment ≠ activation ≠ Circle allocation (FD-036). Unattributed membership is allowed. Seat permanence is **not** guaranteed. Waitlist formula is **Open (OD-023)** — this draft promises no ranking formula.

## 5. Circle formation and Governing Body

Dual status mapping FD-032. Governing Body term six months. Members must not describe 20 members as a “full Circle”. Platform activation credit for Connect BDP is a **BDP** metric, not a member benefit promise.

## 6. Referral functionality

Referrals, if enabled, do **not** guarantee acceptance, Circle seat, or revenue.

## 7. Conduct, suspension, termination, renewal

Members must comply with Circle and platform conduct rules. Suspension/termination follow product due process. Renewal continues only while the membership product remains offered.

**Non-refundable after activation** is the commercial posture (FD-027) subject to exceptional refunds for verified platform/legal errors — **exact matrix OD-007 Open**.

## 8. Business opportunity representations (mandatory)

Logixia / GCE / Connect BDPs must **not** promise, and you acknowledge there is **no**:

- guaranteed referrals  
- guaranteed business  
- guaranteed revenue  
- guaranteed seat permanence  

Illustrative income examples in internal docs are **not** contractual.

## 9. Lead Assist

Stage 1 is **unpaid**. Separate Lead Assist Terms. No ₹500 pay-to-receive, escrow, voucher, success fee, or wallet monetization.

## 10. Data

Privacy Notice. Circle data remains platform assets (FD-034).
