## 1. Structure

This MSA sets framework terms. **Each project is scoped in a Statement of Work (SOW).** Nothing here imposes a universal **30/40/30** milestone split.

## 2. Role of GCE / Logixia (project-specific)

Logixia is **not** always the physical Event executor. The SOW must state whether Logixia is platform, coordinator, contracting intermediary, project manager, service provider, principal, or a combination. **Professional review must determine wording per project.**

## 3. Role of Client

Provide requirements, approvals, access, and timely decisions. Authority proof for signatories.

## 4. Vendors and Enterprise Expert

Vendors may be managed records without a self-serve portal (inactive). Experts work as assigned. Enterprise BDP may be attributed for commission **only** — **no** authority to bind Logixia or execute the project by default.

## 5. Commercial components

Pricing is **componentised**. GCE platform commission/component is distinct from vendor components. EBDP, if any, receives **25% of eligible GCE platform commission**, not 25% of project value.

Internal Finance co-sign when total **proposed** project value is **strictly greater than ₹5,00,000** is a Logixia control — **not** automatically a customer-facing clause.
