## 1. What Lead Assist is

A matching and tracking aid for GCE Connect members. Lead/opportunity data **does not guarantee** successful business, revenue, or closed deals.

## 2. What this is not

These terms **do not** include, and the product must not charge:

- ₹500 pay-to-receive  
- escrow  
- forfeiture  
- voucher  
- subscription credit  
- success fee  
- wallet monetization  
- automatic GCE commission on closed business  

Those flags remain OFF unless a later Founder Decision reactivates them.

## 3. Routing and privacy

Circle-first routing. Contact details hidden until authorised reveal. Opportunity Desk may review for quality/abuse with **minimum necessary** access and audit. Dual confirmation of closed business does **not** post finance in Stage 1.

## 4. AI

Classification may use AI with deterministic fallback. **No model training** on your Lead content until OD-010 is decided and disclosed.

## 5. Conduct

No harvesting contacts for spam; no fake Leads; no off-platform abuse of revealed data.
