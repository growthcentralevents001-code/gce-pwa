## 1. Who we are

Logixia Solutions Private Limited operates the GCE platform (GCE Connect, GCE Marketplace, GCE Enterprise). GCE is a master brand / operating division, not evidenced in this repository as a separate company.

## 2. Data we collect (actual product)

Identity (name, email, phone, account ids); business profile (company, category, specialisation, Tags); KYC/verification records where required for BDP, Venue, Enterprise, payout — **Aadhaar is not mandatory by default**; payment references and invoice data (**not** full card PAN/CVV); bookings, tickets, QR credential metadata; Offer claims/redemptions; Connect Circle, Leads, contact reveal, outcomes; Enterprise client/project/commercial information; operational audit, support, and security records.

We do not seek to operate advertising pixels on the current website (none found in application code as of 15 Aug 2026).

## 3. Purposes

Create and secure accounts; provide memberships, Events, Offers, Enterprise projects; verify businesses where needed; take payments when enabled; prevent fraud; comply with law; improve reliability (error monitoring). **Live marketing automation is OFF** unless a later Founder-approved opt-in exists.

## 4. Consent and other grounds

Counsel must confirm which processing is consent-based versus other grounds available under the DPDP Act **when those sections are in force**. Account creation and checkout should present this notice. Marketing, if ever enabled, requires a **separate** choice — not pre-ticked.

## 5. Sharing / processors

Hosting and database (Supabase); application host; payment provider (Razorpay is a **candidate**, not enabled); error monitoring (Sentry); email/SMS providers **if** live messaging is later enabled; AI providers for Lead Assist classification with a **no-training** default until OD-010 is decided. Venue Partners see what they need to check in / redeem. Opportunity Desk sees Lead queue under internal controls. We do not sell personal data as a product.

Locations of processors: **UNKNOWN — confirm**.

## 6. Retention

Periods are **TO BE CONFIRMED BY LEGAL/CA**. ICT logs may need 180 days under CERT-In directions (28 Apr 2022) where applicable. Destructive purge is gated (`retention_enforcement` OFF).

## 7. Security

Access controls, RLS, encryption in transit as implemented; ticket/claim display tokens stored as AES-256-GCM ciphertext in a dedicated table (Phase 14B). No security measure is a guarantee.

## 8. Rights (when applicable under commenced law)

Access/information, correction, updating, erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent where processing is consent-based, nomination if provided by Rules, and grievance. We will not promise rights broader than the then-current framework. Identity verification will be required.
